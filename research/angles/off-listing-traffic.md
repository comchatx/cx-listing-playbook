## What this covers

**Stage.** All three stages, but from outside the listing. This file covers traffic and conversion that arrive at, or skip, the detail page through doors the listing does not control. These are Brand Stores, Posts (discontinued), Brand Tailored Promotions, Manage Your Customer Engagement emails (discontinued), Amazon Live, Creator Connections and influencer storefronts, Amazon Attribution and the Brand Referral Bonus, Sponsored Brands (including video) and Sponsored Display (now "display ads"), and external deal sites and claim codes. For each one it says what Amazon's own data shows: whether its visits show up in Sales and Traffic sessions, in Search Query Performance (SQP) impressions and clicks, or nowhere the seller's credentials can read.

**Skills it feeds.**
- `effect-review`: the confounder list. A rise or fall in sessions or unit session percentage in an observation window may come from a Store campaign, a livestream, an influencer post, a tailored promotion, an external code or a deal-site spike. None of these is a listing change.
- `listing-review`: no credit and no blame. A listing must not be praised or blamed for a traffic change whose source is off the listing. The detail page can also change with no seller write when influencer videos appear on it.
- `/listing-watch`: the seller's standing-instruction file is where the seller writes down the off-listing activity that cx cannot read from any API (for example a tailored promotion, a livestream or a creator campaign).

**Why a senior operator cares.** Sessions in Business Reports have no traffic-source breakdown. A senior operator reads a session jump with flat search impressions as "something outside search happened", and asks "what did marketing run that week?" before judging the title change. On day 1 of the beta run cx had no method at all (`office-mailbox/from-engineering/20261013-0904-beta-browser-listing-run-day-1.md`), and the roadmap's confounder list (`ceo-office/drafts/2026-10-09-roadmap-candidates.md`, §1.1, "效果评估的纪律") names price, ad budget, stock, coupons and deals, season, review count and Amazon's own traffic allocation. It does not name any of the brand-owned channels below. Two of the channels the brief names no longer exist: Posts ended in July 2025, and Manage Your Customer Engagement stopped sending on 2024-02-16. A skill that asks a seller about them as live tools would date itself.

## Amazon's own rules and guidance

### A. What a "session" counts, and what it never shows

- **RULE (definition).** Sales and Traffic sessions count visits by "any user", whatever the source: "Sessions are visits to your Amazon.com pages using browser or mobile app by any user. All activity within a 24-hour period is considered a session." [official] (sellerSalesAndTrafficReport.json, https://github.com/amzn/selling-partner-api-models/blob/main/schemas/reports/sellerSalesAndTrafficReport.json, read 2026-10-14). Neither the report schema nor the Data Kiosk seller analytics dataset has any traffic-source, channel, referrer or ad dimension. A search of `analytics_sellerAnalytics_2025_03_31.graphql` for external, referral, channel, sponsored, organic, Brand Store, attribution and followers found only `fulfilmentChannel`. [official] (analytics_sellerAnalytics_2025_03_31.graphql, https://github.com/amzn/selling-partner-api-models/blob/main/schemas/data-kiosk/analytics_sellerAnalytics_2025_03_31.graphql, read 2026-10-14)
- **RULE (definition).** The Business Reports glossary says "Sessions- Total" are "Visits to your Amazon product detail pages by a customer within a 24-hour period", and "A storefront or browse node visit by a user is not counted as a session in Business Reports." For page views: "a storefront or browse node visit does not count as a page view", and "When a child ASIN is visited, it does not also count as a visit to the parent ASIN." [official] (Business reports glossary, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G27691, version 7, read 2026-10-14)
  - So a Brand Store visit is **not** a session. A shopper who goes from the Store to a detail page **is** one, and nothing in the session tells where that shopper came from.
- **RULE (definition).** The Business Reports FAQ says: "Since March 29, 2022, Page views and Sessions metrics include mobile app and browser traffic data". It also says "A week runs from Sunday through Saturday", and the "by date" reports are "updated every hour with the data from the past few days". [official] (Business Reports FAQ, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G28761, version 7, read 2026-10-14)

### B. What SQP and Search Catalog Performance count, so off-search traffic is visible by its absence

- **RULE (definition).** SQP "includes organic and Sponsored Products originating from the search results page for all customers. It excludes traffic from widgets on the search page such as "Top Rated " or "New Arrivals."" Also: "An impression is counted every time the ASIN is displayed in the search results page." And: "we do not recommend comparing the metric names of data across different dashboards." [official] (Search Query Performance dashboard, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G8J4CB5ZBF3NX7TP, version 5, read 2026-10-14; capture capture `G8J4CB5ZBF3NX7TP-v5`)
  - Scope of that sentence, read whole from the capture: "The search queries are selected based on their overall performance in search results, and includes organic and Sponsored Products originating from the search results page for all customers." Its grammatical subject is how queries are selected; the page does not repeat the organic-plus-Sponsored-Products wording in its own impression answer ("An impression is counted every time the ASIN is displayed in the search results page"). Amazon's three sources say it in three ways: the SQP help page (selection includes organic and Sponsored Products), the SQP report schema (silent on sponsored; every count "originate[s] from the search results page") and the Search Catalog Performance schema ("Impressions include sponsored search results", ad type not named). None says SQP is organic-only. Files that call Amazon silent on this or the help page unreachable (`angles/paid-keywords.md`, `angles/search-rank-and-volume.md`) predate the capture.
- **RULE (definition).** In the SQP report schema every impression, click, cart-add and purchase field is one that "originate[s] from the search results page". In the Search Catalog Performance schema, impressions are counted "every time the ASIN displays in the search results page. Impressions include sponsored search results." [official] (sellingPartnerSearchQueryPerformanceReport.json; sellingPartnerSearchCatalogPerformanceReport.json, https://github.com/amzn/selling-partner-api-models/tree/main/schemas/reports, read 2026-10-14)
- **RULE (request shape).** For SQP and Search Catalog Performance: "dataStartTime must be a Sunday for the WEEK reportPeriod" and "dataEndTime must be a Saturday for the WEEK reportPeriod", "or else a fatal error returns". [official] (same schemas, re-read 2026-10-14 at reconciliation; `angles/paid-keywords.md` Open question 2, which says the Sunday–Saturday week is not in the schema, is wrong on this point). The Sales and Traffic report schema treats a misaligned WEEK differently: the start "will be expanded to include the first day of the reporting period", which "for the WEEK dateGranularity is Sunday", and the end to the Saturday. [official] (sellerSalesAndTrafficReport.json, above). So the two reports can be asked for the same Sunday–Saturday weeks; only SQP and Search Catalog Performance refuse other bounds.
- **What Amazon does not say.** No page read says whether **Sponsored Brands** (including Sponsored Brands video) placements on the search results page count as SQP impressions. The help page names only "organic and Sponsored Products". Sponsored Display, Brand Store, Amazon Live, influencer, email and external traffic do not originate from the search results page, so by the definition above they are outside SQP. A shopper who comes back later and searches is the exception (see D, branded search).

### C. Brand Stores

- **GUIDANCE / facts.** "Creating and maintaining a Brand Store is free"; Stores are "available for sellers enrolled in Amazon Brand Registry, vendors, and agencies". Shoppers reach a Store "from the byline on your product detail pages or from Sponsored Brands or display ad campaigns", by a short URL, and "Brand Stores are also discoverable through shopping results on Amazon." Amazon recommends: "Update your Brand Store at least once per quarter". Amazon's claims, sourced "Amazon internal data, August 2024": "New-to-brand shoppers who visited a Brand Store were 62.7% more likely to purchase", and Store visitors "purchase 53.9% more frequently" with "a 71.3% higher average order value". [official] (Brand Stores: Create a brand page on Amazon for free | Amazon Ads, https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/stores, read 2026-10-14). These are Amazon's comparisons of visitors with non-visitors, not experiments. They show selection, not effect (see Published research).
- **Store insights definitions** (an older Amazon Stores help page whose metric rows carry "Available from" dates from 2017 to 2018): "Visitors" are "Calculated based on daily unique users or devices"; "Sales" are "Estimated total sales generated by store visitors within 14 days of their last visit." Traffic sources are "Amazon Sponsored Brands", "Amazon organic traffic" ("Traffic originating from your brand link on Amazon product detail pages"), "Your tags" and "Other sources". [official] (Metrics and traffic sources | Stores, https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/01/AmazonStores/Help/en/Stores_Insights/Metrics_and_sources.html, read 2026-10-14)
  - Note: Store insights' "Amazon organic traffic" is traffic that came **from the listing's own byline**. A Store's organic visits are partly the listing's own traffic going round.
- Attribution in Store insights: "We use a last discovery methodology"; "We attribute the sale to the last source of the customer visit prior to their purchase"; halo sales are "products available on Amazon from your brand, where the product purchased was not available on the store page viewed"; and "we display sales data in Stores insights based on the date of the visit, not the date the order was placed." [official] (Orders, sales and units sold | Stores, https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/01/AmazonStores/Help/en/Stores_Insights/Orders_sales_and_units.html, read 2026-10-14)
  - A Store's sales figures therefore land on the visit date, while Sales and Traffic units land on the order date. The two cannot be lined up day by day.
- Source tags: "a URL parameter, ?channel=tag-name"; "Only the top 100 tags by visitors for the time range viewed will be broken down"; tag names limited "to 20 characters". [official] (Tagged sources | Stores, https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/01/AmazonStores/Help/en/Stores_Insights/Tagged_sources.html, read 2026-10-14)
- Sponsored Brands reports carry `brandStorePageView`, "The number of Brand Store pages that were viewed and attributed to your ads" (report type `sbCampaigns` only). [official] (Reporting columns (version 3), https://advertising.amazon.com/API/docs/en-us/guides/reporting/v3/columns, read via its Markdown source, 2026-10-14)

### D. Sponsored Brands (and video) and Sponsored Display as session sources

- Sponsored Brands ads "appear in prominent placements like top of search and product pages, driving traffic to your Brand Store or detail pages". In campaign set-up, "Under "Landing page," select whether you want to drive shoppers directly to your Brand Store or to a product detail page." A Sponsored Brands ad "will be reviewed within 72 hours." [official] (Sponsored Brands – Increase brand awareness | Amazon Ads, https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/sponsored-brands, read 2026-10-14)
  - Consequence for sessions: a Sponsored Brands click that lands on a detail page is a session. One that lands on the Store is not a session until the shopper opens a detail page (A).
- **Sponsored Display is renamed.** "Sponsored Display is now known as display ads"; it is "part of Amazon's wider display ads offering within our ad platform, Amazon Ads Agent"; "Existing Sponsored Display campaigns will continue to run uninterrupted." No date is given on the page. [official] (Sponsored Display - Now part of Amazon Ads display ads offering, https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/sponsored-display, read 2026-10-14)
- **Ad-attributed figures use 14 days, click or view, for Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display.** `detailPageViews`: "Number of detail page views occurring within 14 days of an ad click or view"; `purchases`: "within 14 days of an ad click or view"; `brandedSearches`: "The number of searches that included the name of your brand occurring within 14 days of an ad click or view" (report types sb*, sd*, st*); `video5SecondViews`: "Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Products video-only metric". [official] (Reporting columns (version 3), above)
- **The view-attribution model changed on 2026-01-01.** "We are introducing a shopping-signal enhanced last-touch attribution model on January 1, 2026"; it "applies a shorter attribution window". It affects "Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display campaigns billed on a viewable impression (vCPM) basis, including branded keyword campaigns" and Amazon DSP in the Store. "Click-based attribution remains unchanged." The 14-day all-views figures remain as "'all views' metrics (e.g., Purchases (all views))". [official] (View Attribution Updates for Amazon Store ads | Amazon Ads, https://advertising.amazon.com/resources/whats-new/view-attribution-updates-for-amazon-store-ads, dated January 01, 2026, read 2026-10-14)

### E. Posts (discontinued)

- "Posts were discontinued in July 2025". "New Post creation was disabled in June 2025". Posts "were a browse and discovery experience that allowed brands to showcase their products through lifestyle imagery, videos, and captions across various feeds on Amazon." The alternatives Amazon names are Brand Stores and Amazon Live. [official] (Amazon Posts Discontinued – Explore Alternative Solutions | Amazon Ads, https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/posts, read 2026-10-14)

### F. Manage Your Customer Engagement (discontinued)

- "The Manage Your Customer Engagement (MYCE) tool for brands is no longer available and stopped sending campaigns on February 16, 2024." "Any MYCE campaigns that were still sending as of February 16, 2024, will only reach a portion of the intended send population." The named replacements are "Brand Tailored Promotions and Brand Customer Reviews". [official] (Seller Forums News and Announcements, "Manage Your Customer Engagement is no longer available", News_Amazon, datePublished 2024-03-07, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/9334fd74-a331-4d8e-85a0-0cc74609ab66, read 2026-10-14)
- The help page a 2021-era forum thread links for MYCE (GF85G8KLPFJZZV5K) returns no topic through the content call. [official, negative] (content call, 2026-10-14)

### G. Brand Tailored Promotions

- **RULE (eligibility).** Brand Tailored Promotions are for sellers "that have a Brand Representative selling role assigned in Brand Registry". Products with no reviews are eligible. "Products with 1-4 reviews must have an average rating of 2.5 stars"; "Products with 5+ reviews must have an average rating of at least 3 stars"; products must be "New". [official] (Brand Tailored promotions, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/GFM3F4GG5EYCC5XC, version 10, read 2026-10-14)
- **RULE (audience and cap).** "You will be eligible to create tailored promotions only for an audience size of 1,000 or more"; "You can have a maximum of 20 active or scheduled promotions at any given time"; "There is no fee". The page lists audiences grouped by goal (Customer Acquisition, Customer Retention, Re-engagement, Cross-sell) with data refresh frequencies from daily to monthly. Examples: "Brand followers" ("Customers who have clicked to follow your brand on Amazon", daily), "Repeat customers" (monthly) and "Views remarketing" (daily). [official] (same)
- **Where it shows, and to whom.** "Eligible customers (audiences) will be able to view the promotion on search and detail pages, promotion shopping page, as well as on the Brand Stores page", with a green badge reading like "Save X% with brand promotion XXXX". "The promotion shows only to the select audience". It may not show if "The tailored promotion is not the highest value promotion" or "Your product might not be the Featured Offer". [official] (same)
- **Measurement.** The dashboard metrics are "Sales", "Spends" and "Redemptions". "No, currently we don't generate a detailed order-level or ASIN-level report for tailored promotions." Tailored promotions "may combine with other unrestricted promotions, such as deals and coupons, that do not have claim codes"; and "Budget overspending is most likely to occur during high-traffic events". [official] (same)
- Launch announcement (US, 2023): "available to all brand owners enrolled in the Brand Registry operating in Amazon's U.S. store", "promotional discounts ranging from 10% to 50% off", "limited to one unit redemption per customer", "six audience types". [official] (Brand Tailored Promotions: Offer exclusive discounts for loyal customers, Benjamin Hartman, sell.amazon.com blog, Aug 1, 2023, https://sell.amazon.com/blog/announcements/brand-tailored-promotions, read 2026-10-14). The 2026 help page lists more audiences than the six at launch.

### H. Amazon Live

- **RULE (eligibility).** Amazon Live is "available to professional Amazon sellers and vendors who are registered in Amazon's Brand Registry with a Brand Representative or Brand Rights Owner role, have an approved and published brand store, and sell on Amazon.com." Streams play "directly on your Brand Store". The Live Shopping tile's visibility is set "up to 60 days". "Only one Live Shopping tile is supported per Brand Store page". The tile may take "up to 48 hours" to appear. [official] (Amazon Live guide, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/GBD2VGB79N7X5WTX, version 1, read 2026-10-14)
- **Measurement.** Metrics are in the Amazon Live Creator App. "Metrics begin populating 72 hours after your livestream ends"; channel-level data covers "the previous 180 days". Amazon advises: "Wait for at least two weeks before conducting a deep performance analysis." Attribution types are "Direct", "Halo" (in-brand products not promoted) and "Total". [official] (same)
- **Where it plays.** Live content "can be integrated into your Brand Store, Amazon.com/live mobile and web experiences", the FAST channel on Prime Video and Fire TV, and "targeted media and social content". "Amazon.com/live mobile and web experiences is only available in India and the U.S." Amazon's claim: audiences exposed to Live creative plus another ad product "showed a 55% higher branded search rate" ("Amazon internal data, US, May 1–Oct 31, 2024", 11 campaigns). [official] (Amazon Live - Create shoppable live advertising streams | Amazon Ads, https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-live, read 2026-10-14)
  - Branded search is a path from Live (and from Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display, D) **into** SQP. A livestream can raise branded-query volume and the brand's SQP impressions without any listing change.

### I. Creator Connections and influencer storefronts

- **Influencer storefronts (Associates Central help).** "The Amazon Influencer Program allows you to get your own presence on Amazon"; it comes with "a special vanity URL (amazon.com/shop/handle)". [official] (Amazon.com Associates Central - Help, "What's the Amazon Influencer Program?", https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GTP6NKQ2GXPZL7AT, read 2026-10-14)
- **Influencer content can appear on the seller's detail page.** "Once you've uploaded 10 videos approved by moderation and completed sign up for onsite commissions, shoppers may also find your videos on relevant product detail pages on Amazon for products you feature in your videos." [official] (Associates Central help, "How do I upload videos to my storefront?", https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GKZN7G85Q4XGX29C, read 2026-10-14). "Onsite Earnings": "shoppable content and product recommendations you upload to your Amazon storefront may be selected to appear to customers as they search and browse Amazon". [official] (Associates Central help, "Earning Onsite Commissions", https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GV7VUCCSS8MQFSQT, read 2026-10-14)
  - The detail page a shopper sees can therefore gain third-party video content with no write by the seller.
- **Creator Connections.** An Amazon staff post says Creator Connections "allows you, as a brand owner, to partner with established content creators from the Amazon Associates program", to "Drive traffic to your product listings". [official, weak: forum post] (Seller Forums, "Amazon Creator Connections", Xander_Amazon, datePublished 2025-01-31, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/eb63894f-3b29-447a-a7ec-e7ebd82ce17a, read 2026-10-14). An Amazon staff reply in another thread: "The creator Connections access for Sellers is currently closed." [official, weak: forum reply by Roberto_Amazon, shown as "2 years ago"; page data near it carries 2024-05-31] (Seller Forums, "Creator Connections Access (Seller Central)", https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/bb21a715-56e0-4f8a-843e-b6fb5a4ef58e, read 2026-10-14). No Amazon help page with Creator Connections attribution rules could be read (see Pages read).

### J. Amazon Attribution and the Brand Referral Bonus

- **Amazon Attribution measures non-Amazon media.** It covers "non-Amazon channels including search, social, display, video, email, affiliate/influencer campaigns, etc." It "is a free measurement solution". Reports "have a 14-day attribution window". Metrics are clicks, detail page views, add-to-cart, purchases, units, product sales and new-to-brand. [official] (Amazon Attribution: Get started with advertising analytics | Amazon Ads, https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-attribution, read 2026-10-14)
- **RULE (attribution).** "only links with Amazon Attribution tags from that profile are considered for conversion attribution and the last-clicked tag in the 14-day window gets full credit. Conversions will not be attributed to Amazon ads or any other links not tagged by the given profile." "Clicks to Product Detail pages auto-associate that product and its variations"; "Clicks to Amazon Stores pages auto-associate products listed on the Stores page". "Total" and "brand halo" metrics cover "non-promoted products in the same brand". [official] (Amazon Attribution API how-to, https://advertising.amazon.com/API/docs/en-us/guides/amazon-attribution/how-to, Markdown source read 2026-10-14)
- **Reporting limits.** Performance report dates: "Last 13 months". Products report: "Last 90 days", "limited to the top 100 products per campaign". Rows with fewer than 10 lifetime clicks are masked: "masked rows will show metric values as zero". The `brb_bonus_amount` metric is returned only when asked for, with `groupBy` `CAMPAIGN` or `ADGROUP`. [official] (same). The overview page still calls Attribution "currently available in beta" in nine markets. The product page lists more countries. [official] (Amazon Attribution API overview, https://advertising.amazon.com/API/docs/en-us/guides/amazon-attribution/overview, read 2026-10-14)
- **Brand Referral Bonus.** It "credits Brands an average of 10% of sales from traffic you have driven to Amazon"; "The bonus applies to brand-owned products with an Amazon Attribution tag that originates from a non-Amazon marketing effort", plus same-brand purchases "up to 14 days after clicking on the ad". Eligibility is a Brand Representative selling in Amazon's US store. The bonus report is generated "every Friday", covers "the trailing 365 days", and is not generated with no qualified sales in 90 days. The bonus is credited after "two months". [official] (Brand Referral Bonus, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/GL9HPJ34VBFP76HX, version 10, read 2026-10-14). "The bonus isn't available for Amazon advertising (like sponsored or video ads)." [official] (Brand Referral Bonus: Drive traffic and boost your bottom line, Katelynn Gonzalez, sell.amazon.com blog, Oct 20, 2023, https://sell.amazon.com/blog/brand-referral-bonus, read 2026-10-14)

### K. External deal sites, claim codes and the conduct rules around them

- **RULE (policy).** Unfair activities include "Manipulating sales rank (such as by accepting non-authentic orders or orders that you have paid for, or refunded externally or orders that you discounted externally)"; "Artificially inflating web traffic (using bots or paying for clicks, for example)"; and "Attempting to influence search results by inflating search ranking through keyword manipulation or incentivizing customers' searches to appear as organic behavior". [official] (Selling policies and seller code of conduct, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G1801, version 8, read 2026-10-14)
- **Claim codes are the sanctioned off-Amazon discount.** "Claim codes help you restrict your promotion to be redeemed by only those customers who have the claim code in their possession"; "When you go with a single-use or group claim code, we will not show those codes on the product detail page." [official] (Use claim codes for a promotion, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G202140170, version 3, read 2026-10-14). Amazon's promotions page advises: "Consider running social media campaigns to drive traffic to your promotion." [official] (Amazon promotions, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G60951, version 11, read 2026-10-14)
- **Where redemptions show.** The promotion Tracking ID appears in the Orders report and the Payments report, which add fields such as `Item-Promotion-Discount` and `Item-Promotion-ID`. [official] (Promotions and Orders report, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G43531, version 2, read 2026-10-14)
- **Associates.** "Associates drive internet traffic to Amazon.com through specially formatted links"; "Associates earn up to 10% in referral fees". [official] (The Amazon Associates Program, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G28591, version 1, read 2026-10-14). Deal sites that link to Amazon are typically Associates, and the seller does not control them. That last point is the seat's reading, not a page's words.

### L. Amazon's own marketing selection

- Amazon may feature ASINs in seasonal events and "Some ASINs may even be selected for inclusion in Amazon social media and influencer content". Selection depends on "marketing readiness" criteria: brand registered, optimized title, high-resolution main image, "4+ images", "3+ bullet points", keywords enabled, star rating, FBA inventory health. "we can't guarantee that your products will be included in events. Requirements are subject to change". [official] (Unlock marketing opportunities, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/GFJTFMCXPRQ5GHUK, version 2, read 2026-10-14)
  - An Amazon-run placement or social post is a session source the seller may not know happened.

## What practitioners add

- **Attributed is not incremental.** [opinion] David Lopes, Polar Analytics, "Amazon Attribution: What It Tracks, What It Misses, and How to Read It", 2026-08-17: "attributed revenue is not incremental revenue"; under last touch "every earlier touchpoint is worth zero"; the tool "stops at the marketplace boundary". The published research below agrees.
- **Attribution sales look organic in Business Reports.** [opinion] Lazar Zepinic, guest on Helium 10 podcast #114, "A PPC Expert Shows How to Put Amazon Attribution to Work for You", 2020-02-18 (modified 2020-10-06): "all of the Attribution sales are going to be registered there as organic", and "Amazon likes it when you bring external traffic". No source is given for the ranking claim, and no Amazon page read states that external traffic improves organic rank. **Disagreement with Amazon:** Zepinic describes the 14-day window as covering all sales in the period. Amazon's API guide says "the last-clicked tag in the 14-day window gets full credit".
- **Creator Connections mechanics.** Practitioners agree on minimums, and Amazon publishes none on a page that could be read:
  - [opinion] Reed Thompson, Goat Consulting, "Amazon Creator Connections Sellers Working with Influencers" (published 2023-12-27, modified 2026-04-02): "The current program minimums require a minimum budget of $5,000 and a minimum commission rate of 10%", found "under the Brand Content side tab" of the Ads console.
  - [opinion] David Katz, Archer Affiliates, "Amazon Creator Connections Explained (for Sellers)", 2024-08-24: it "uses Amazon Associates' 24-hour attribution window", versus Amazon Attribution, which "tracks conversions over a 14-day window". Archer lists "deal sites" among the creator types.

## Published research

- [research] Thomas Blake, Chris Nosko, Steven Tadelis, "Consumer Heterogeneity and Paid Search Effectiveness: A Large-Scale Field Experiment", *Econometrica* 83(1):155–174, 2015 (NBER WP 20171, 2014). Narrow finding, from the NBER abstract: in eBay field experiments, "returns from paid search are a fraction of conventional non-experimental estimates", and "brand-keyword ads have no measurable short-term benefits." Transfer: branded traffic (Store visits from the byline, branded searches after a livestream) is largely traffic that would have come anyway. Attributed figures overstate the effect. This is eBay and Google paid search, not Amazon.
- [research] Randall A. Lewis, Justin M. Rao, "The Unfavorable Economics of Measuring the Returns to Advertising", *Quarterly Journal of Economics* 130(4):1941–1973, 2015. Narrow finding (abstract via Crossref): across 25 large field experiments "The median confidence interval on return on investment is over 100 percentage points wide", and "selection bias, due to the targeted nature of advertising, is a crippling concern for widely employed observational methods." Transfer: one seller's before/after cannot measure a campaign's small effect. It can only rule a large confounder in or out.
- [research] Brett R. Gordon, Florian Zettelmeyer, Neha Bhargava, Dan Chapsky, "A Comparison of Approaches to Advertising Measurement: Evidence from Big Field Experiments at Facebook", *Marketing Science* 38(2):193–225, 2019. Narrow finding (Crossref abstract): "Observational methods often fail to accurately recover the treatment effects generated from randomized advertising experiments on Facebook." Transfer: Amazon's "visitors vs non-visitors" figures (Stores, Live) are observational. They are not effect sizes.
- [research] Kay H. Brodersen, Fabian Gallusser, Jim Koehler, Nicolas Remy, Steven L. Scott, "Inferring causal impact using Bayesian structural time-series models", *Annals of Applied Statistics* 9(1):247–274, 2015. Narrow finding (Crossref abstract): a synthetic-control state-space model can estimate an intervention's effect over time and "accommodate… the time-varying influence of contemporaneous covariates". It is illustrated on "the causal effect of an online advertising campaign on search-related site visits". Transfer: a campaign running inside a listing's observation window is exactly such a contemporaneous covariate. It must be modelled or excluded, not ignored.

## Stable enough for a skill

- Sales and Traffic sessions count detail-page visits from any source and carry no traffic-source breakdown. (sellerSalesAndTrafficReport.json; Business reports glossary; analytics_sellerAnalytics_2025_03_31.graphql)
- A Brand Store visit is not a session or a page view in Business Reports. A detail-page visit that follows it is a session. (Business reports glossary)
- SQP counts only what originates from the search results page. Amazon's help page says its search-results basis includes organic and Sponsored Products and excludes widgets; no Amazon source calls it organic-only. Store, livestream, influencer, email, display and external traffic are outside it. (Search Query Performance dashboard; sellingPartnerSearchQueryPerformanceReport.json; sellingPartnerSearchCatalogPerformanceReport.json)
- Sessions rising while SQP impressions and clicks for the ASIN stay flat points to traffic from outside search. (seat's inference from the two definitions above; the definitions are Amazon's)
- Posts were discontinued in July 2025 and Manage Your Customer Engagement stopped sending on 2024-02-16. Neither is a current traffic source. (Amazon Posts Discontinued page; News_Amazon forum post)
- Brand Tailored Promotions show only to the chosen audience, on search, detail pages and the Brand Store. Amazon gives no ASIN-level or order-level report for them. (Brand Tailored promotions help page)
- Amazon Attribution credits the last tagged click within its window, counts only tagged non-Amazon links, and masks rows under a click threshold. (Amazon Attribution API how-to)
- The Brand Referral Bonus applies only to Attribution-tagged non-Amazon traffic, never to Amazon ads. (Brand Referral Bonus help page; Brand Referral Bonus blog)
- Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display report detail page views, purchases and branded searches that follow an ad click or view within an attribution window. Their view-based attribution model changed on 2026-01-01, so ad-attributed figures across that date are not comparable. (Reporting columns (version 3); View Attribution Updates for Amazon Store ads)
- Influencer videos can appear on a product's detail page with no write by the seller. (Associates Central help, storefront videos and onsite commissions)
- Amazon's conduct policy forbids manipulating sales rank with externally discounted or refunded orders and incentivizing searches to look organic. (Selling policies and seller code of conduct)
- Claim-code promotions are not shown on the detail page, and their redemptions carry a promotion ID in the Orders report. (Use claim codes for a promotion; Promotions and Orders report)
- Amazon's own comparisons of Store or Live visitors with non-visitors are observational, not effects. (Gordon et al. 2019; Lewis & Rao 2015)

## Positions

- **P-off-listing-traffic-1** — Before crediting or blaming a listing change for a move in sessions or unit session percentage, cx asks the seller, or reads the seller's standing-instruction file, for off-listing activity in both windows: Brand Store changes, Sponsored Brands or display campaigns, Amazon Live streams, Brand Tailored Promotions, claim-code or social promotions, creator campaigns and external or deal-site pushes. Sessions carry no source that could rule these out. (sellerSalesAndTrafficReport.json; analytics_sellerAnalytics_2025_03_31.graphql; Business reports glossary)
- **P-off-listing-traffic-2** — cx reads a session change beside the same ASIN's SQP impressions and clicks for the same reporting weeks, with the period bounds each report's schema requires. A session change without a matching search change is attributed to traffic from outside search, not to the title, bullets or search terms.
