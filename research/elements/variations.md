# Element: variations (parent-child, variation themes)

Read 2026-10-14 by the research seat. Tags: [official] Amazon's own page or document; [opinion] a named practitioner; [research] published research; [seat] the seat's own reasoning, never a skill position.

## What this covers

**Stage: conversion first, exposure second.** A variation family puts several buyable children (sizes, colours, pack counts) on one detail page under a non-buyable parent. It serves **conversion**: the shopper picks an option without leaving the page, and the page's star rating and review count can be pooled across children. It serves **exposure** less directly. Amazon's own seller blog says purchases of any variation "can collectively improve the ranking for your product". Practitioners say search normally shows one child per family. An Amazon Science preprint names "search deduplication" as one use of variant relationships. Click-through is touched only through the rating and review count shown on the search tile: since February 2026, Amazon says that tile reflects only the reviews eligible to be shared.

**Skills it feeds:**
- **listing-review.** Read the family's structure. Name a misfit child or a theme that does not describe the real difference. Notice when the review pool is split. Treat a competitor's split or merge as a trigger.
- **listing-change-log.** A family change is a change of many listings at once. Its "before" must hold the whole family: parent SKU, child SKUs, theme, each child's theme attributes, and Catalog relationships.
- **effect-review.** A family change moves the denominator of every parent-level metric. It can move the displayed rating and review count. It is a confounder for any other change made during the same window.

**Why a senior operator cares:**
1. Variation changes are the listing changes most likely to be irreversible or slow to reverse. Amazon's API guide documents only one way to remove a relationship: delete the parent or the child listing.
2. Since 2026 the review pool is decided by Amazon from the variation theme. A wrong theme can strip reviews from children, and a corrected theme can restore them.
3. Grouping functionally different products under one parent breaks Amazon's variation rules, and using a family to lend one product's ratings to another has drawn an FTC order (The Bountiful Company, 2023) that bars the company from creating variation relationships.
4. Amazon says new listings must avoid a deprecated theme value (existing listings may keep it). Amazon's first theme-removal post (2025-08-19) also said an update to a listing with a deprecated theme fails with "the value specified is invalid"; its revision narrowed the removal and does not repeat that error. The theme cleanup of August 2025 shows Amazon changes the theme lists by announcement.

## Amazon's own rules and guidance

### What a variation family is

- A family has three parts: parent, children, and variation theme. The parent is "the non-buyable product that appears in search results and relates child products"; the children are "The buyable products related to the parent product"; the theme is "The relationship between the parent and child products", each category having its own themes, colour and size given as common examples. The post names the categories that allow variations as including "Pet Supplies, Toys & Games, Sports & Outdoors, Grocery, Health & Beauty" and "Clothing, Accessories & Shoes". [official] (How to create Amazon product variations, Mickey Toogood, Amazon, published 2024-12-31, https://sell.amazon.com/blog/amazon-variation-listing, read 2026-10-14)
- **RULE:** "A parent listing must not be a buyable product." The parent is "a non-buyable entity used to relate child products". [official] (Building Listings Management Workflows Guide, https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/building-listings-management-workflows-guide, read 2026-10-14; the `developer-docs.amazon.com` address redirects there)
- The Catalog summaries mark a parent as `itemClassification` `VARIATION_PARENT`: "A parent catalog item that groups child items into a variation family". A child is `BASE_PRODUCT`: "Can be a standalone ASIN or a variation child item". [official] (catalogItems_2022-04-01.json, https://github.com/amzn/selling-partner-api-models, read 2026-10-14)

### Review sharing across variations: the 2026 change

**Announcement (RULE, Amazon-enforced).** Amazon announced it on 2026-01-07. Thread wording: "Starting February 12, 2026, we're changing how reviews are shared across products in a variation." Reviews are now shared "only between variations with minor differences that don't affect functionality".

**Rollout.** "gradually by product category between February 12, 2026, and May 31, 2026", with "an email notification 30 days before any changes affect your products".

**Types that keep sharing:**
- "Colour or pattern variations of the same product"
- "Size variations that maintain the same function" (example: king and queen bedding)
- "Pack size or quantity variations"
- "Secondary scent variations for non-scent-focused products"
- "Different model fitments for the same product type" (example: phone cases)

**Types that stop sharing.** "Reviews will no longer appear for variations with significant differences" (UK wording; the US thread says "on variations"), which "may affect your products' overall star ratings and review counts" (US). The phrase "significant differences in features or specifications" appears in the UK thread's description of the old behaviour, which shared reviews even across such differences.

**What sellers are asked to do.** Check the family in Manage All Inventory "to ensure that they accurately reflect product differences". Use the right theme: "using colour variation for colour differences, not quantity variation". After a theme is corrected, "reviews will be re-shared for eligible products". The UK thread links help pages G200786710 (listing quality), G8831 (variation relationships) and G74DLBUEHT5GY88C (review sharing guidelines). [official] (Seller Forums, News_Amazon, UK thread "Changes to review sharing across product variations starting from Feb 12", https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/83ba401b-1295-44be-97b1-4ce263ab3c06, and US thread "Changes to review sharing across product variations starting Feb 12", https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/a572986d-9bb1-4e91-a53f-41c575874dd7, both read 2026-10-14; the pages show only "9 months ago"; the 2026-01-07 date comes from the follow-up thread below)

**Follow-up announcement** (News_Amazon, "Check review sharing eligibility with Seller Assistant"):
- "review sharing eligibility is determined by variation theme attribute". Amazon's example: "the flavor variations won't share reviews, but the size variations will."
- "If it's determined that variations are being inconsistently used and include significant differences between products, reviews won't be shared across any product variations." This is the severe case: the whole family loses pooling.
- "The star ratings and review count on the search results page will reflect only the reviews that are eligible" to be shared. This touches **click-through**.
- On Best Sellers Rank: "No, this is not impacted by review sharing."
- "Vine reviews will be reinstated to the original child ASINs when applicable, subject to Vine policy limits and compliance with Amazon's review policies."
- Eligibility per variation can be asked of Seller Assistant ("Seller Central's conversational assistant"). Amazon names no API for it. [official] (Seller Forums, News_Amazon, "Check review sharing eligibility with Seller Assistant", https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/bf2eb6d6-f6a9-4880-a8f9-9e1fc9d64615, read 2026-10-14; the page shows "6 months ago" and refers to the 2026-01-07 announcement)

### Variation themes: where they live, and the 2025 cleanup

- **RULE (schema).** A parent carries three attributes:
  - `parentage_level` = `parent`
  - `child_parent_sku_relationship` with `child_relationship_type` = `variation`
  - `variation_theme` with a `name` "applicable for your product"

  A child carries:
  - `parentage_level` = `child`
  - `child_parent_sku_relationship` with `child_relationship_type` `variation` and `parent_sku`
  - the same `variation_theme` name as its parent

  "When you select a variation theme for a child listing item, you must provide the corresponding properties." The guide's example theme `SIZE/COLOR/NUMBER_OF_ITEMS` makes `shirt_size`, `color` and `number_of_items` mandatory. "Most product facts must be replicated across all listings within the variation family." [official] (Building Listings Management Workflows Guide)
- **RULE (schema lifecycle).** "enumDeprecated indicates enumeration values that are deprecated and must be avoided". The `variation_theme` values flagged there must not be used by new listings, though existing listings may keep them. [official] (Building Listings Management Workflows Guide)
- **The theme list is per product type and lives in the schema.** `getDefinitionsProductType` returns it.
  - `parentageLevel=PARENT`, `CHILD` or `NONE` returns a schema "simplified by resolving all conditional logic related to the specified parentage level".
  - `NONE` "Eliminates all variation-related conditional logic". The guide adds: "Variation-related attributes are excluded from the returned schema."
  - Amazon added `parentageLevel` on 2026-05-27. [official] (definitionsProductTypes_2020-09-01.json; Building Listings Management Workflows Guide; SP-API Release Notes, https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/sp-api-release-notes, read 2026-10-14)
  - Product type definitions "always describe the latest up-to-date Amazon catalog requirements" unless a `productTypeVersion` is named. `RELEASE_CANDIDATE` is a prerelease. [official] (Product Type Definitions API, https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/product-type-api-use-case-guide; definitionsProductTypes_2020-09-01.json)
- **The theme list changes by announcement.**
  - The first post, "Removal of irrelevant variation themes will start September 2" (News_Amazon; the page shows "a year ago", its page data dates the post 2025-08-19; PPC Land calls it the August 20 announcement): "Between September 2, 2025, and November 30, 2025, we will remove variation themes from our product templates that aren't relevant or frequently used". Impacted themes were already marked "Deprecated: Do Not Use" in the template's Variation Theme Name field. "If you try to update a listing with a deprecated variation theme, you'll receive an error message that states, "the value specified is invalid" and the update will not be successful." Its steps "To update your ASINs with impacted variation themes": delete the existing parent ASIN in Manage Inventory; remove "Parentage Level, Child Relationship Type, Parent SKU, and Variation Theme Name attributes" from the children; "Create a new parent ASIN with an available variation theme"; assign the children to it. "If you're unable to update impacted variation themes, your child ASINs will remain active as stand-alone listings". The post now opens with a pointer to the update thread below. [official] (Seller Forums, News_Amazon, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/29b9e0b4-2b4d-4caa-b4af-83b57c47862d, read 2026-10-14; also quoted in angles/competitor-discovery.md)
  - 2025-08-20 (PPC Land's date for the same first post): Amazon announced that "irrelevant or redundant variation themes would be removed from our listing experiences". These are "marked as 'Deprecated: Do Not Use' in our new product templates".
  - Revision: the list was "revised ... to only remove variation themes that had no sales in the past 12 months."
  - For sellers: "Your existing variation families will continue to operate normally, with no disruption to sales"; "Child ASINs will stay active even if you choose to update your variation themes."
  - The revision does not say whether the "the value specified is invalid" error still applies to the themes it kept or to the themes it still removes. Which theme values are live for a product type is read at run time from `getDefinitionsProductType` (`enumDeprecated`), not from either post.
  - The post links the original 2025-08-20 announcement (a Seller News article, `/seller-news/articles/QTJFVVExV1RHQ1RCRzIjR0VaM1RZNlJaS0NMUlJNOQ`, not opened), a spreadsheet "Variation themes planned for removal" (m.media-amazon.com, `Variation_Theme_Planned_for_Removal.xlsx`, not opened), and the Variation relationship FAQ, G201951410. [official] (Seller Forums, News_Amazon, "Update to irrelevant or redundant variation theme removal", https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/0a027b19-0e4f-4525-9535-13aefae92cda, read 2026-10-14; the page shows only "a year ago"; the 2025-08-26 date of this clarification comes from PPC Land, below)

### Changing a family through the API (RULE: documented behaviour)

- **Create.** Create the parent with the schema read at `parentageLevel=PARENT`. Then create each child, one per unique combination of theme attributes, with the schema at `CHILD`. [official] (Building Listings Management Workflows Guide)
- **Move a child.** "You must remove a child listing from its current parent-child relationship before you relate it to a new parent." [official] (same)
- **Remove a relationship.** "To remove a parent-child relationship for a listing item, delete the parent or child listing." "When you delete a parent listing, the relationships between the parent and all of its child listings are removed." "When you delete a child listing, only the relationship between that particular child listing and its parent is removed." Deleting a relationship leaves "the statuses of any remaining listings … not impacted". The guide gives no `patchListingsItem` body that removes a relationship without deleting a listing. [official] (same)
- **Rate.** "Updates that contain relationship updating attributes" have their own row in the limits table, quoted as "Limit of 100 requests per application per second" on `patchListingsItem` and `putListingsItem` (on `patchListingsItem` the product-data row reads the same figure; validation previews have a lower one). The page names `child_parent_sku_relationship` (and `package_contains_sku`) as relationship attributes. The figure is for human readers; the page owns it. The page and the model disagree on some bursts: the page's 2021-08-01 table gives `getListingsItem` and `putListingsItem` a burst of 5, while `listingsItems_2021-08-01.json` gives 10 (rate 5 in both; re-read 2026-10-14). The binding values are the ones Amazon returns in the `x-amzn-RateLimit-Limit` response header, as the model says; this file picks neither. [official] (Listings Items API Rate Limits, https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/listings-items-api-rate-limits, read 2026-10-14)
- **Legacy feed.** The old relationship feed `POST_PRODUCT_RELATIONSHIP_DATA` is in the Feeds deprecation entries of 2024-03-20 and 2024-04-24, and in the 2025-06-25 notice that moved the removal date "from June 30, 2025 to July 31, 2025". [official] (SP-API Release Notes)

### Reading a family through the API

- **Listings Items** `getListingsItem` / `searchListingsItems` with `includedData=relationships`. The reply holds, per marketplace, `type` (`VARIATION` or `PACKAGE_HIERARCHY`), `parentSkus`, `childSkus` and `variationTheme` {`theme`, `attributes`}. The theme is "the combination of listing item attributes that define the variation family". This is the seller's side, keyed by SKU. [official] (listingsItems_2021-08-01.json)
- `searchListingsItems` with `variationParentSku` "Filters results to include listing items that are variation children of the specified SKU". It cannot be combined with `identifiers` or `packageHierarchySku`. [official] (listingsItems_2021-08-01.json)
- **Catalog Items** `getCatalogItem` / `searchCatalogItems` with `includedData=relationships` return `parentAsins`, `childAsins` and `variationTheme`. This is Amazon's catalogue side, keyed by ASIN. Catalog Items 2022-04-01 "Replaced `variations` with `relationships`" (release note 2022-05-18). [official] (catalogItems_2022-04-01.json; SP-API Release Notes)
- **Notifications.** No relationship-change notification type appears among the notification schemas: none of the 26 files under `schemas/notifications` mentions "variation", "parentAsin", "parent_sku" or "relationship" (the listings-side types are item status, issues, MFN quantity, product type change, branded item content and product type definitions). [official] (schemas/notifications in selling-partner-api-models, read 2026-10-14)

**H2. The tools: blank form, spreadsheet, Variation Wizard, and "Fix variation".**
- **Current help pages name two methods.**

## What practitioners add

- **Search shows one child per family.** Amazon "by default defers to what it thinks is the best option for the most conversions". Weaker children benefit by "piggybacking" on the strong one. Exceptions it names: children that differ significantly in design or style, or a niche uncompetitive enough to rank several children on page one. Separate listings risk relegating less popular versions "to oblivion"; the article favours variation listings in most cases. [opinion] (Bradley Sutton, Helium 10, "Is It Better to Add Variations to Existing Amazon Product Pages or Have Separate Listings?", 2020-01-13, modified 2022-03-14, https://www.helium10.com/blog/amazon-product-variations/)
- **Families can harm as well as help.** A grouped page "often has a better chance of ranking higher", but frequent changes (the page's example is UPC changes) "can harm your product rankings and visibility". Flat-file errors "can ruin the relationship". The article's FAQ claims a limit of 2,000 child ASINs per parent; the seat found no Amazon source for it. It also reports a case of conversion rising "from 7% to 22%" after simplifying variations, from a video case study, with no data shown. [opinion] (My Amazon Guy, "Mastering Amazon Variation Relationships: A Complete Guide", page body "Last Updated: September 19, 2025"; no author or first-publication date shown on the page as read, https://myamazonguy.com/amazon-variation-relationships-guide)
- **Theme changes, practitioner version.** To change a theme: delete the parent, clear Parent SKU / Parentage Level / Variation Theme Name on each child, create a new parent, re-link. ## Published research

- **Variant relationships at Amazon.** Herrero-Vidal, Chen, Liu, Sen, Wang (Amazon.com), "Learning variant product relationship and variation attributes from e-commerce website structures", arXiv:2410.02779, 2024, marked "preprint, under review". Variant relationships matter for "having similar, but not identical, products listed on the same webpage or share reviews". Identifying them "can also be exploited for many other applications, such as review sharing or search deduplication". The definition of a variant "can vary across product types or even brands"; the paper's example is that for drinks the difference is "flavor or sugar content" (note: Amazon's 2026 rule stops review sharing across flavour). Affiliations (Amazon.com) and the "preprint, under review, 2024" mark are in the PDF, not on the abstract page. **Transfer:** Amazon engineers describing the purpose (review sharing, search deduplication), not measuring an effect. It supports the practitioner claim that search shows one family member; it does not prove it. [research]
- **Grouping variants automatically.** West, Al Jadda, Ahsan, Qu, Cui, "Interpretable Methods for Identifying Product Variants", arXiv:2104.05504, 2021. A clustering method groups products that differ in a few attributes. **Transfer:** method only; no effect on shoppers. [research]
- **Choice overload, null on average.** Scheibehenne, Greifeneder, Todd, "Can There Ever Be Too Many Options? A Meta-Analytic Review of Choice Overload", Journal of Consumer Research 37(3):409–425, 2010. Across "63 conditions from 50 published and unpublished experiments (N = 5,036)", "a mean effect size of virtually zero but considerable variance between studies". Lab and field choice studies, not online marketplaces. [research]
- **Choice overload, moderators.** Chernev, Böckenholt, Goodman, "Choice overload: A conceptual review and meta-analysis", Journal of Consumer Psychology 25(2):333–358, 2015 (online 2014; abstract read via Crossref). A meta-analysis of 99 observations (N = 7,202): four moderators (choice-set complexity, decision-task difficulty, preference uncertainty, an effort-minimising goal) each have "a reliable and significant impact on choice overload", higher levels facilitating it. [research]
- **Choice overload, the original study.** Iyengar & Lepper, "When choice is demotivating", Journal of Personality and Social Psychology 79(6):995–1006, 2000. Only the citation was read; no finding is taken from it here. [research]
- **What the choice-overload work means for families.** [seat] Amazon's "don't overwhelm customers" fits a conditional effect, not a rule; many children are plausibly a risk mainly when options are hard to compare. This is the seat's reading of the two meta-analyses, not a finding of either about Amazon.
- **Ratings move sales.** Chevalier & Mayzlin, "The Effect of Word of Mouth on Sales: Online Book Reviews", Journal of Marketing Research 43(3):345–354, 2006 (abstract via Crossref). Amazon.com vs Barnesandnoble.com: "an improvement in a book's reviews leads to an increase in relative sales at that site", and for most samples one-star reviews have a greater impact than five-star ones. **Transfer:** a rating or count change from a review split can plausibly move conversion. Books, 2006, cross-site sales-rank design; not a variation study. [research]
- **No study found** that measures the effect of pooling or splitting reviews across variants on conversion. [research: none found]

## Stable enough for a skill

1. A family is a non-buyable parent plus buyable children joined by one variation theme; a parent must never be buyable. (Building Listings Management Workflows Guide; How to create Amazon product variations)
2. Functionally different products and newer generations belong on separate detail pages, not in one family. (How to create Amazon product variations)
3. The themes allowed for a product type, and each theme's required attributes, live in that product type's schema from `getDefinitionsProductType`; read it at the listing's parentage level. (Building Listings Management Workflows Guide; definitionsProductTypes_2020-09-01.json)
4. A theme value flagged `enumDeprecated` must not be used for a new listing; Amazon prunes themes by announcement. (Building Listings Management Workflows Guide; "Update to irrelevant or redundant variation theme removal")
5. A child must leave its family before it joins another, and Amazon's documented way to remove a relationship is deleting the parent or the child listing; deleting the parent unlinks every child. (Building Listings Management Workflows Guide)
6. Since the 2026 change, whether children share reviews is decided by Amazon from the variation theme attribute: minor non-functional differences share, significant ones do not, and inconsistent use can stop sharing across the whole family. (News_Amazon threads, review sharing)
7. The star rating and review count on the search results page reflect only the reviews eligible to be shared; Amazon says Best Sellers Rank is not affected by review sharing. (News_Amazon, "Check review sharing eligibility with Seller Assistant")
8. After a wrong theme is corrected, eligible reviews are re-shared. (News_Amazon, review sharing thread)
9. The seller's side of a family (SKUs) and Amazon's catalogue side (ASINs) are read separately, and they can disagree. (listingsItems / catalogItems models; the disagreement itself is [seat])
10. A variation page pools ratings and badges across its children, which is why linking a different product into a family to borrow them has drawn an FTC order barring the company from creating such relationships; Amazon's own rule is that functionally different products are listed separately. (FTC Charges Supplement Marketer with Hijacking Ratings and Reviews on Amazon.com…; How to create Amazon product variations)
11. Every child of a family shares the parent's product type, brand and the other non-varying attributes (such as item type keyword, model name, style); a child that differs in one of them cannot join. (Product variations overview; Create and manage product variations)
12. The detail page belongs to Amazon's catalogue, not to a seller. Other sellers can add valid children, and Amazon may add, move or split children and whole families on its own. A seller's delete removes only the seller's own offer and relationship, so a child that others also offer can stay in the family. (Troubleshoot product variations; Variation relationship FAQ)
13. A broken, hijacked or wrongly split family is escalated to Selling Partner Support (directly or through Seller Assistant) with the ASINs and the reason. Support decides, and may delete a child whose attributes or images do not fit. (Troubleshoot product variations; Variation relationship FAQ)
14. Amazon's help pages say a family's theme cannot be changed after creation; the documented correction is a new family with the right theme and the children moved to it. Amazon's 2025 theme-removal post gives the same rebuild as the way to "update" a theme (delete the parent, clear the children's parentage and theme attributes, create a new parent, re-assign); the review-sharing guideline speaks of "updating" themes without saying how. (Variation relationship FAQ; Troubleshoot product variations; Removal of irrelevant variation themes will start September 2; Guidelines for reviews shared across product variations)
15. The Featured Offer is decided separately for each child, and a visit to a child is not counted as a visit to the parent. (Becoming the Featured Offer; Business reports glossary)
16. Whether children share one Best Sellers Rank with the parent or each child ranks alone depends on the parent's website display group; where the parent ranks, Best Seller badges are not shown on the detail page. (Sales Rank FAQ)
17. In Clothing, Accessories & Luggage, Sporting Goods and Beauty the detail page shows the parent's title and description and the shopper picks a child; in all other categories the best-selling child's page is shown. The order of children is Amazon's and cannot be changed by the seller. (Variation display and customer experience; Variation relationship FAQ)
18. The swatch image has its own slot (`SWCH`, `swatch_product_image_locator`) and takes no text or price; without it the child's main image is the thumbnail. (Product image guide; Submit media attributes)
19. Apparel and footwear sizes are structured attributes (a size system tied to the store, a size class, the size and optional range end, and body or height types where required) that Amazon assembles into the displayed size; the names and allowed values live in the product type schema. (Guidance for Complex Attributes; Shoe size standards; Apparel size standards)
20. Brand-specific size charts are for brand-registered sellers, can be set at the parent and inherited by all children, and Amazon suppresses size-chart images in the image block in favour of its own generated chart. (Add size charts with Selling Partner Support; Size chart self-serve tool; Size chart image improvements)
21. Adding or assigning a child to a family after a deal is submitted makes the deal revalidate and can suppress or cancel it. (Amazon deals)
22. No SP-API notification announces a relationship change. A family change is seen only by reading Catalog Items `relationships` (and the Listings `relationships`) again and comparing. (Notification Type Values; schemas/notifications; catalogItems_2022-04-01.json)
23. In Sponsored Products reports, a purchase of a sibling after a click on one child's ad is reported as other-SKU sales of the advertised product, and a variation parent has its own ad-eligibility status (`VARIATION_PARENT`). (Reporting columns (version 3); Purchased product reports; Eligibility 3.0 OpenAPI)
24. Amazon publishes no appeal path for a review-sharing decision; the stated remedies are Seller Assistant's eligibility check and re-sharing after a corrected theme. (Guidelines for reviews shared across product variations; News_Amazon review-sharing threads)

## What cx must read at run time

- **Schema, before any family write:** `getDefinitionsProductType` for the child's product type, marketplace, `requirements=LISTING`, `parentageLevel=CHILD` (or `PARENT` for the parent, `NONE` for a standalone listing). Read the `variation_theme` enum and its `$lifecycle.enumDeprecated`, and the attributes the chosen theme makes required. Amazon's guide shows the request shape.
- **The seller's family:**
  - `getListingsItem` with `includedData=relationships,attributes,issues,summaries` for the SKU.
  - `searchListingsItems` with `variationParentSku=<parent SKU>` for all children.
  - Attributes to read: `parentage_level`, `child_parent_sku_relationship`, `variation_theme`, and each child's theme attributes (for example `color`, `size`).
- **Amazon's family:** `getCatalogItem` for the parent and child ASINs with `includedData=relationships,summaries`. Read `parentAsins`, `childAsins`, `variationTheme`, and `summaries.itemClassification`. Compare with the Listings side.
- **Result of a write:** the `issues` returned by `patchListingsItem`, which can first be run as `mode=VALIDATION_PREVIEW`. Then `getListingsItem` `issues` once Amazon has processed it, and Catalog `relationships` to see the family Amazon actually holds.
- **Figures (report names, options and waits are door.md's; door.md is the box's file, not a research file):**
  - Sales and Traffic via `createReport` / `getReport` / `getReportDocument`, at `asinGranularity` `CHILD` for per-child sessions and conversion, `PARENT` for the family.
  - Data Kiosk (`createQuery`): the seller analytics dataset's per-ASIN `displayRating` and `totalRatingCount` to see a review split; `salesAndTrafficTrends` for child series.
- **Review-sharing eligibility:** no API. Seller Central's Seller Assistant answers it. The seller can put that answer into the tree.
- **Keepa (any ASIN, including competitors)** — Keepa's API documentation, not Amazon's:
  - `productType` (`5` = variation parent, no product data), `parentAsin`, `parentAsinHistory` (previous parent ASINs with the time each ceased).
  - `variations`, parent ASINs only. Keepa's sources disagree on the cap: Product.java says "dimension attributes for up to 50 variations". The web Product Object page says "Contains up to 4000 variations", "Only available variations are provided", and live data needs `offers`. *Gap fill:* both re-read 2026-10-14 (again on reconciliation); the disagreement is Keepa's own, and angles/competitor-set.md records the same. cx uses neither cap: the live answer is the array a read returns, and a list of exactly 50 is treated as possibly cut. A raw `/product` on a parent with more than 50 children settles it.
  - `websiteDisplayGroup` (tells whether rank is shared with the parent; see H8), and `monthlySold` ("variation specific"; the bought-past-month figure in brackets).
  - A parent references itself in `parentAsin` ("a parent product references itself in parentAsin", web Product Object page).
  - `historicalVariations` (requires a `historical-variations` parameter that the box's raw `/product` route does not list).
  - `isRedirectASIN` (offers parameter).
  - `reviews` (variation-specific rating and count history; "ratingCount history has not been updated since April 9th 2025") versus csv slots 16/17 (non-variation-specific). Keepa's Product.java says `monthlySold` "is variation specific". *Gap fill:* slot 17 is `COUNT_REVIEWS`. The web page calls it "The product's rating count history" and Product.java calls it "The product's review count history". - **Detecting a family change (no notification exists, H11).** One scheduled read per day per watched family:
  1. `getCatalogItem` with `includedData=relationships,summaries,salesRanks` for the parent ASIN and each known child ASIN, per marketplace.
  2. Store, per ASIN: `parentAsins`, `childAsins`, `variationTheme.theme`, `variationTheme.attributes`, `summaries.itemClassification`, and the `displayGroupRanks` group.
  3. Compare with the previous day's file. Any change is a family event:
     - a child gone from `childAsins`
     - a new child
     - a child whose `parentAsins` changed
     - a theme or attribute-list change
     - a parent's classification change
  4. For the seller's own SKUs, the same comparison on `searchListingsItems` with `variationParentSku`, and `getListingsItem` `relationships` and `issues`. A Catalog change with no Listings change means someone other than the seller, or Amazon, changed the family.
  5. For competitors' families, Keepa's `parentAsinHistory` dates the change after the fact.
  6. Brand owners can also subscribe to `ITEM_PRODUCT_TYPE_CHANGE`. Amazon changing a child's product type is a plausible cause of a break.

- **Ads (only once the Ads API is connected):** the Eligibility API (`POST /eligibility/product/list`) for each child; `VARIATION_PARENT` and `NOT_IN_BUYBOX` statuses. The `spAdvertisedProduct` and `spPurchasedProduct` reports for same-SKU vs other-SKU sales by advertised and purchased ASIN.
- **Rank display:** Catalog `salesRanks` per child and parent, and Keepa `websiteDisplayGroup`, before reading a "BSR change" as a child's own movement.
- **Size data:** the product type schema at `parentageLevel=CHILD` for the size attributes (system, class, value, range end, body or height type) the theme needs.

## Open questions

1. **Does a `patchListingsItem` `delete` on `child_parent_sku_relationship` unlink a child without deleting it?** Amazon's guide documents only listing deletion, while practitioners clear the parent fields in flat files. *Settle:* a `VALIDATION_PREVIEW` on a sandbox or a seller-approved SKU, then Catalog `relationships` after processing.
2. **Does a re-linked child keep its own reviews and sales history?** Does a new parent ASIN reset parent-level history? *Gap fill: partly.* G201951410 says "Existing ASINs may already have sales history and reviews … Using existing ASINs preserves this history". That is about the child ASIN, not the parent's series. G8841 is not served. *Settle:* the first real family change observed in Catalog and Data Kiosk.
3. **What exactly do the review sharing guidelines (G74DLBUEHT5GY88C) say?** That covers the full list of non-sharing themes, "inconsistent use", and appeals. *Gap fill: settled for the list and appeals* (H10, and the flavour note above). The page states no appeal. The guideline page does not use the word "inconsistent"; the whole-family rule ("being inconsistently used and include significant differences") is stated only in the News_Amazon threads quoted above.
4. **Is there an API answer for review-sharing eligibility?** Seller Assistant is the only channel found. *Settle:* SP-API release notes after 2026-10 and the Data Kiosk seller analytics docs.
5. **Is Search Query Performance's `asin` option child or parent level?** No Amazon page states it. angles/search-query-performance.md records Amazon staff, speaking on a Helium 10 episode, describing child-level credit, and leaves a parent in `asin` open. *Settle:* the first SQP run on a child ASIN and on its parent.
6. **Does Amazon restrict who may create or edit a family (Brand Registry ownership)?** *Gap fill: partly settled.* After Brand Registry, "only you and other sellers associated with your brand can create or suggest updates" to detail pages (blog). Only the rights owner can request an unmerge of brand-registered ASINs (G201950610). Amazon's catalogue owns the page, and other sellers can change families (H4). *Still open:* whether a non-brand seller's addition of a child to a brand-registered family is blocked or only reviewed. *Settle:* a Brand Registry help page read by a signed-in brand owner.
7. **Is there a children-per-parent limit?** The 2,000 figure is a practitioner claim. *Gap fill: settled.* Amazon's pages say 4,000 children are the display limit (H1).
8. **Which `issues` codes signal a variation problem** (theme attribute missing, parent buyable, deprecated theme)? *Settle:* the first real `getListingsItem` on a parent and child.
9. **Can Amazon Ads advertise a parent ASIN?** How do ads attribute across children? *Gap fill: partly settled.* Sibling purchases are other-SKU sales of the advertised SKU (H9). A parent has the eligibility status `VARIATION_PARENT`, but the model does not say whether its severity is `INELIGIBLE` or `ELIGIBLE_WITH_WARNING`. *Settle:* one `POST /eligibility/product/list` on a parent ASIN once the Ads API is connected.
10. **The original theme-removal announcement and the spreadsheet of removed themes.** *Reconciliation: partly settled.* The first forum post (2025-08-19) was read: the 2025-09-02 to 2025-11-30 window, "Deprecated: Do Not Use", the "the value specified is invalid" error and the rebuild steps are Amazon's (angles/competitor-discovery.md had them right). *Still open:* the Seller News article it points to, the "Variation themes planned for removal" spreadsheet, and whether the error applies to themes kept after the revision. *Settle:* Seller Central signed in for the article and spreadsheet; `getDefinitionsProductType` `enumDeprecated` for the live values.
11. **Theme change: allowed or not?** The help pages say a theme cannot be changed. The 2025 and 2026 announcements and an older moderator speak of updating themes (H1). The 2025 first post's "update" is the rebuild under a new parent; whether an in-place replace through the API is accepted is still open. *Settle:* a `VALIDATION_PREVIEW` `patchListingsItem` that replaces `variation_theme` on a seller-approved parent, and the `issues` it returns.
12. **Is the Variation Wizard still live?** Its help page is gone in seven layers, while Amazon's blogs (2024-12 and 2026-04) still send sellers to it. *Settle:* the seller opening `/listing/varwiz`, or Catalog > Add Products, on the first run.
13. **Do the SP-API listings reports (`GET_MERCHANT_LISTINGS_ALL_DATA`, `GET_MERCHANT_LISTINGS_DATA`) carry a parent ASIN or parentage column?** The Seller Central FAQ says the Active and All Listings reports hold "Parent ASIN details". The SP-API page describes no such column. *Settle:* the first downloaded report (door.md's operation names).
14. **Does a merge or re-parenting move a child's "bought in past month" badge or Keepa `monthlySold`?** Amazon's only word is "unique customers who purchased the ASIN in the past 30 days" (moderator), and Keepa says the value is variation specific. Nothing covers merges. *Settle:* Keepa `monthlySoldHistory` across a recorded family change.
15. **Is review sharing enforced in every marketplace?** Announcements were found for US, UK, DE and ES. The guideline page exists only in the US layer. *Settle:* News_Amazon threads on .co.jp, .in, .com.br and others, or Seller Assistant asked in that marketplace.
16. **Is there any appeal for a review-sharing decision beyond re-sharing after a theme fix?** None stated (H10). *Settle:* a Selling Partner Support case on a first real split, with the answer recorded.
17. **Which Amazon figure is right for a family change to show:** 15 minutes, 2 hours, 24, 24–28, 24–48 or 96 hours (H5)? *Settle:* the first recorded family write, timing Catalog `relationships` and the displayed rating against the receipt.
18. **Search display.** Does a clothing-type family's search tile index the parent or a child? Amazon's display page and the "My ASIN is unsearchable" page disagree (H6). *Settle:* a search read where the tile's ASIN is visible, together with Catalog `itemClassification`.
19. **Does Amazon auto-merge families ("AVS") when product type and size match?** Only seller reports relay it. *Settle:* an Amazon page or staff reply naming it, or a recorded Catalog diff showing a merge nobody submitted.

## Positions

1. **P-variations-1:** Before proposing any family change, cx reads the seller's side (`getListingsItem` `relationships` and `searchListingsItems` `variationParentSku`) and Amazon's side (Catalog `relationships`) and records the whole family in the change log's "before".
