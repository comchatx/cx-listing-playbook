# Element: A+ Content, Brand Story and Premium A+

Read 2026-10-14 by the research seat. Tags: [official] Amazon's own page or document; [opinion] a named practitioner; [research] published research; [seat] the seat's own reasoning, never a skill position.

## What this covers

**Funnel stage: conversion.** A+ Content sits on the detail page, below the buy box, in the product-description slot. A shopper sees it only after clicking through, so it can move conversion (unit session percentage) and, through Brand Story and the comparison modules, cross-sell to the brand's other ASINs. It does not appear in the search results, so it cannot move click-through. Whether it moves exposure (search indexing) is the disputed question below. Amazon's own words place it in conversion: "Create listings that convert with rich content that features product details… at a key point in the shopper journey" (Quickguide, 2026), and Amazon presents it as reducing returns and negative reviews (Complete Guide 2023).

**Skills fed:**
- **listing-review**: where A+ sits in the funnel; how to read what is live (A+ Content API, Keepa `aPlus`); what a clean A+ state looks like (`APPROVED`, ASIN badge `CONTENT_PUBLISHED`, no `warnings`/`errors` on validation); that A+ hides the plain-text description; that A+ is *not* a Listings Items attribute.
- **listing-change-log**: an A+ change has a different receipt (a `contentReferenceKey`, a status that moves DRAFT → SUBMITTED → APPROVED/REJECTED, a moderation lag measured in days) and can touch many ASINs at once.
- **effect-review**: the observation window starts at publication, not at submission. A+ is one of the few elements Amazon's own A/B tool (Manage Your Experiments) tests, and a running experiment is a confounder.

**Why a senior operator cares:** A+ is the element agencies sell hardest, and Amazon backs it with its own "up to 8% / up to 20%" claim. An operator knows these things, and a playbook that misses them is wrong in front of them:
- the claim is a marketing figure from "Amazon internal data" with a changing footnote;
- moderation takes days;
- publishing a new document silently replaces the old one;
- A+ text is probably not keyword-indexed on Amazon;
- the plain description stops showing once A+ is live.

The day-2 run showed this gap. The cx run with no method listed "A+" among the changes it would make "by PATCH to this path", meaning the Listings Items `PATCH /listings/2021-08-01/items/<sellerId>/<SKU>` (internal run report 20261014-0921: cx's own proposal, quoted in the report, notes that its items 2, 3, 5, 6 and 9 — images, A+, bullet points, shipping group — are all "PATCH 此路径", i.e. a PATCH to this path). That route does not carry A+. A+ has its own API.

Content work is out of the first round (roadmap §1.1 boundary: images and A+ need publicly reachable URLs). The skill still has to know where A+ sits so that it neither proposes A+ through the wrong door nor blames A+ for a click-through drop.

## Amazon's own rules and guidance

### What A+ is, its three kinds, and where it shows

- A+ Content is "collections of modules (known as a content document) that you can use to display product images and text." [official] (A+ Content API, https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/a-plus-content-api-use-case-guide, read 2026-10-14)
- **There are three content types.** `EBC` is "A+ Enhanced Product Description content published through the A+ Content Manager in Seller Central". `EMC` is the same for Vendor Central. `BrandStory` is "A+ Brand Story content, which helps customers gain a better understanding of the brand." [official] (model `aplusContent_2020-11-01.json`, `ContentType`, https://github.com/amzn/selling-partner-api-models/blob/main/models/aplus-content-api-model/aplusContent_2020-11-01.json, read 2026-10-14)
- **Where it renders.** Premium (and Standard) seller content appears "under **Product Description** for Seller Content (EBC) or under **From the Manufacturer** for Vendor Content (EMC)". Brand Story appears "under the 'From the Brand' heading — a dedicated space completely separate from your product-level A+ Content." [official] (A+ Premium Modules, https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/a-plus-premium-modules; A+ Brand Story Modules, https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/a-plus-brand-story-modules; both read 2026-10-14)
- **A+ replaces the plain description on the page.** "A+ content will replace the current plain-text product description. Ensure that your A+ content includes all the necessary details from the plain-text product description field if you feel that they are needed." [official] (Complete Guide to A+ Content for Brand Owners, UK edition, PDF created 2021-12-05, https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/02/Sell/guide/Complete_Guide_to_A_UK.pdf, read 2026-10-14) The current help page restates it (gap fill): "your A+ Content will hide the current plain-text product description … Even though the plain-text product description is not shown, it is still best practice to make sure that changes you make to A+ Content are also reflected in that field as well". [official] (A+ Content, G202102930 v17, read 2026-10-14)
- **One description-type document per ASIN.** "Only one enhanced product description type can be live on a product listing at a time." [official] (A+ Content quick guide, v1.1 "040126", PDF created 2026-04-01, https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/01/bx-marketing/Quickguide_aplus-content.pdf, read 2026-10-14)
- **What each kind is for.** Basic is for sellers "just getting started, or [with] simple products that don't require extra details". Premium is for "similar products for shoppers to compare, or … products with a lot of details or tech specs". Brand Story is for "building brand awareness". [official] (Quickguide, as above) Brand Story "is meant to complement A+ Content; while Premium Content focuses on product features to drive sales, Brand Story expands the customer's knowledge of your brand and provides cross-sell opportunities." [official] (A+ Brand Story Modules)

### Eligibility (RULE, enforced at create/submit)

- **The public page's rule.** You need a Professional selling account, and "the Brand Representative or Reseller role assigned to a brand enrolled in Amazon Brand Registry, or have generic products in your catalog". "You can only apply A+ Content to your brand-owned ASINs." A+ "is free for sellers who meet eligibility requirements." [official] (A+ Content | Sell on Amazon, https://sell.amazon.com/tools/a-content, read 2026-10-14)
- **Enforcement in the API.** "Validation and ASIN relations are permissive. Ownership, brand eligibility and account entitlements are enforced when the document is created or submitted." An ASIN outside the brand is refused with 403 `Unauthorized`: "You are unable to add content to this ASIN because our system does not recognize this ASIN as part of your brand." Brand Story without Brand Registry is refused with "User [<merchantId>] shouldn't be using project type: [BrandStory]". [official] (A+ Content Troubleshooting Guide, https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/a-plus-troubleshooting, read 2026-10-14)
- **Amazon Retail can hold the ASIN.** "You are unable to add content to this ASIN because there is an existing retail contribution on this ASIN." The seller must ask Selling Partner Support to release the ASIN, and "the API cannot override it." [official] (Troubleshooting Guide)
- **Premium A+ eligibility: the current Seller Central help page states it (gap fill, 2026-10-14).** The page "A+ Content" (G202102930, version 17, revision 0, layer Published/US, status Live), under "Premium A+ FAQ", says the criteria are "for any country that you sell and publish A+ Content in. When you meet eligibility in one country, you will be given Premium A+ access worldwide." You are eligible if:
  - "You have already published A+ Brand Story to all ASINs that you own within your catalog. Essentially, all ASINs within your A+ Content Manager need to have a published Brand Story."
  - "You have had at least 5 project submissions of A+ Content that have been approved and published within the past 12 months."

  "Every week, we will re-evaluate the eligibility … If you are eligible, we will enable this feature for you within the A+ Content Manager and you will see a banner". On cost: Premium is "available to you at no cost during this promotional period"; "If we announce a fee for Premium A+, you will be given the opportunity to opt-in for a fee. We will not charge you for any fees without your consent." At the end of the promotional period "we may disable your ability to add Premium A+ modules to new ASINs", while Premium content already published "will remain on the detail page free of charge". [official] (A+ Content, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G202102930, read 2026-10-14 through the help hub content call described in method/help-page-access.md)
  - **This supersedes the older statement.** A News_Amazon forum post, "Brands: New Premium A+ content promotion", dated only "4 years ago", set "at least 15 A+ content projects approved in the past 12 months", counted "for each Amazon store", plus "published A+ brand story across your ASIN catalog", with access "granted at the end of each month". [official, dated, superseded] (Seller Forums, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/56714bc31667d6e317a4aa85bee089f1, read 2026-10-14) The live help page differs on four points: 5 not 15, approved *and published*, a weekly not a monthly re-evaluation, and eligibility in one country grants access worldwide rather than per store.
  - **A second current Amazon document agrees on the count, with different wording (reconciled 2026-10-14).** Amazon's Global Selling deck in Turkish (PDF metadata: created 2026-03-13) says: "UYARI: Premium A+ Content açabilmek için son 12 ayda en az 5 onaylı Temel A+ Content ve tüm ürünlerinizde Brand Story olmalıdır" (Warning: to open Premium A+ Content you must have at least 5 approved *Basic* A+ Content in the last 12 months and Brand Story on all your products). [official] (Amazon.com Ürünlerine A+ Content Oluşturma Rehberi, https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/41/SOA/tr2us/TR2US_AContent.pdf, read 2026-10-14; also cited by description.md) The two Amazon sources differ in wording: the deck counts "approved Basic A+ Content", the help page "project submissions of A+ Content that have been approved and published". Neither is preferred here.
  - The figures (5, 12 months, weekly) are Amazon's and may change; the binding answer for a given seller is read at run time: the banner in A+ Content Manager and the API's 403/400 on a Premium submission.

### Modules and limits (RULE in the model; figures for human readers, they live in the model and change by release)

- **Release of September 30, 2026.** The A+ Content API "Added support for the 19 Premium A+ content modules and five Brand Story content modules through the same operations used for Standard A+ Content", and added `createMedia`, `getMedia` and `updateMedia`. [official] (SP-API release notes, https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/sp-api-release-notes, read 2026-10-14)
- **The model's `ContentModuleType` enum** holds 15 `STANDARD_*`, 19 `PREMIUM_*` and 5 `BRAND_STORY_*` values. [official] (model) The Quickguide's own table says 14 Basic, 19 Premium and 4 Brand Story module types. It also gives "Max modules per project" as 5 Basic, 7 Premium and 19 Brand Story. [official] (Quickguide) The Seller Central help table and the Turkish Global Selling deck also say 14 Basic and 19 Premium templates. [official] (A+ Content, G202102930 v17; Amazon.com Ürünlerine A+ Content Oluşturma Rehberi) **Amazon's sources disagree on the type counts:** the model has 15 Standard and 5 Brand Story types where the seller documents say 14 and 4 (the release note of 2026-09-30 says "five Brand Story content modules", agreeing with the model). The binding list is the model's `ContentModuleType` enum, read at run time; the skill states no count.
- **No mixing.** "Modules within a content document must be entirely Standard or entirely Premium." The error is "Content documents cannot contain both premium and standard module types." Brand Story modules "cannot be combined with Standard or Premium modules in the same content document". [official] (A+ Premium Modules; Troubleshooting Guide; A+ Brand Story Modules)
- **Amazon's pages disagree on the module cap.** The tutorial says "Content documents for selling partners can contain up to seven modules; content documents for vendors can contain up to five". [official] (Create, edit, and publish A+ content, https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/create-edit-publish-aplus-content, read 2026-10-14) The troubleshooting guide says the 7-module cap "applies as soon as the list contains one Premium module; Standard-only documents are not capped by the API". [official] (Troubleshooting Guide) The seller-facing guides say Basic allows five: "You can add up to five Basic A+ Content modules per ASIN… up to seven modules per ASIN with Premium." [official] (How to create A+ Content: Design tips, guidelines, and examples, Daisy Quaker, 2025-09-24, https://sell.amazon.com/blog/a-plus-content-design-guide, read 2026-10-14) The API is the enforcement. The limit a skill must trust is the `errors` of `validateContentDocumentAsinRelations`. (Gap fill: the Seller Central help table gives "Allowable Modules on Detail Page" as 5 for Basic and 7 for Premium [official] (A+ Content, G202102930 v17). So Seller Central, the Quickguide and the blog agree on 5/7, and only the developer troubleshooting guide says the API does not cap Standard-only documents. - **Every Brand Story needs a background card.** Every Brand Story document "must include exactly one `BrandStoryImageWithLogoModule`". [official] (A+ Brand Story Modules)
- **Per-field limits are in the model and the module pages.** Examples:
  - Premium image-text headline 1–80 characters, body ≤ 500;
  - Premium FAQ 2–5 pairs, question ≤ 120, answer ≤ 250;
  - Premium text module up to 5,000 characters;
  - the three Premium comparison modules require `productAsin` per column.

  Amazon's recommended use for the comparison modules is "differences between products in your catalog". [official] (A+ Premium Modules) The Premium FAQ module's stated use includes "Address common concerns that lead to customer complaints or returns". [official] (A+ Premium Modules)

### Content policy (RULE: rejection in moderation; the Seller Central guideline page was read in the gap fill, see "Category claims and takedowns" below)

- **The seller-facing restrictions, as Amazon's own blog states them:**
  - "Substantiate awards or endorsements you reference with certifying body and year."
  - "Don't include claims like "best-selling" or "top-rated" in your text or images."
  - "Don't include warranty or guarantee information in text placements."
  - "Don't include pricing, promotional, shipping details, QR codes, hyperlinks", nor contact information.
  - "Never reference or compare products with competitors."
  - "Avoid using low-resolution or animated images."
  - "Image sizes should be no larger than 2MB."
  - "Avoid embedding text in images so mobile customers can read all the details on smaller screens."

  [official, guidance-level page stating rules] (How to create A+ Content: Design tips…, sell.amazon.com blog)
- **The lead "comparisons with competitors are prohibited" holds.** The blog says so in the words above. The KDP version of the guideline says "Do not reference or compare products with competitors, regardless of how competitors are mentioned". [official, KDP program] (A+ Content Guidelines, https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G4WB7VPPEAREHAAD, read 2026-10-14)
- **The KDP guidelines** are Amazon's A+ guideline text for books. They are the only full guideline list Amazon serves publicly, and the seat read it as a near relative of the seller list, not as the seller list. Its rules:
  - no prices or promotional words ("affordable", "free", "bonus");
  - no customer reviews;
  - no guarantees;
  - awards need the body and year, and none older than two years;
  - no time-sensitive words ("now", "new", "latest");
  - no links, QR codes or contact details;
  - images under 2 MB ("Individual files must remain under 2 MB"), not reused from the image gallery;
  - alt text that "does not describe the image and that would not be useful for a customer using a screen reader application can result in content rejection";
  - no claims to "cure, mitigate, treat, or prevent diseases"; certification claims ("recommended by, certified, tested…") need a note naming the certifying body;
  - "Violating these guidelines may lead to a rejection by our system, potential removal of your ASIN, or both."

  [official, KDP] (A+ Content Guidelines, KDP) Whether every KDP line holds for sellers is an open question.
- **Images must follow the general image rules.** "Images must comply with Amazon's product image requirements" (help G1881). [official] (Create, edit, and publish A+ content) G1881 is the Seller Central page "Product image guide" (version 28, revision 9), read on 2026-10-14 through the help hub content call; its text is captured at capture `G1881-v28`, and its rules are owned by images-and-video.md (method/help-page-access.md, C1).

### Moderation, timing and statuses (RULE for statuses; timing is guidance)

- **The four statuses** (enum `ContentStatus`):
  - `DRAFT`: "has not yet been submitted for approval".
  - `SUBMITTED`: "submitted for approval and is currently waiting for moderation".
  - `APPROVED`: "approved and will be published to applied ASINs".
  - `REJECTED`: "rejected in moderation and needs to be revised and resubmitted based on the rejection reasons provided".

  [official] (model) The model has no field that carries rejection reasons (a search of the whole model for "reason" hits only that enum description and the generic 403 text "Possible reasons include Access Denied, Unauthorized, Expired Token, or Invalid Signature"). The Seller Central guide says to "view the rejection reasons by clicking on 'Edit'". [official] (Complete Guide 2023 US, below)
- **Timing.**
  - "Review can take up to seven business days (longer during periods of high volume)".
  - "After the A+ Content has been approved, it can take up to 24 hours to be published on the detail pages".
  - "If you resubmit content after being rejected, it will also take up to seven business days for review."

  [official] (Complete Guide to A+ Content for Brand Owners, v1.1, "Last updated March 28, 2023", https://storage.pardot.com/229492/1680539631VYq9kP6F/AMZ_Guide_A__04b_US__1_.pdf, linked from go.amazonsellerservices.com/aplusbpg_EN_202303, read 2026-10-14)
- **Some accounts skip review.** "Some accounts are approved automatically. Poll until `APPROVED` or `REJECTED`; do not wait for `SUBMITTED`." [official] (Troubleshooting Guide)
- **Polling.** "Call the `getContentDocument` operation no more than once an hour for each content document that you check for approval status." [official] (Create, edit, and publish A+ content)
- **Silent replacement, three ways.**
  - "When you publish a content document to an ASIN that already has content published to it, Amazon suspends the existing content from the ASIN." [official] (Manage A+ content, https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/manage-aplus-content, read 2026-10-14)
  - "Approving another document of the same family on the same ASIN takes over the publish record silently"; the replaced document's badge reads `CONTENT_NOT_PUBLISHED`. [official] (Troubleshooting Guide)
  - "Posting ASIN relations to an approved document, or updating it, resets the status (200, no warning)… The approved revision stays live until the new one is approved." [official] (Troubleshooting Guide)
- **The ASIN set is replaced whole.** "The ASINs that you post replace all ASINs related to a content document… Removing the ASIN can suspend the content document from the ASIN. Post the entire set of ASINs". [official] (Manage A+ content)
- **A 200 is not a pass.** "A 200 status code in the response indicates that Amazon ran a validation on your content document. This status code does not indicate that your content document passed validation." [official] (Create, edit, and publish A+ content) Likewise, `postContentDocumentAsinRelations` "returns 200 for any ASIN, an empty set included"; an unusable ASIN shows up only as `ASIN_FAILED_VALIDATION` and a badge (`BRAND_NOT_ELIGIBLE`, `CATALOG_NOT_FOUND`) on `listContentDocumentAsinRelations` with `includedDataSet=METADATA`. [official] (Troubleshooting Guide)
- **Content badges** are `BULK`, `GENERATED` ("generated by an automated process. If any user modifies this content, it will lose the `GENERATED` badge"), `LAUNCHPAD`, `PREMIUM` and `STANDARD`. The tier is read from `badgeSet`: "Publish records carry the content family, not the tier". [official] (model; Troubleshooting Guide)
- **Locale.** A+ shows only in the language chosen: "if you publish content in German, it will only appear on the detail page when the German language is selected". [official] (Complete Guide 2023 US) The API wants `en-US` (hyphen); publish records report `en_US`. [official] (Troubleshooting Guide)
- **Copying across countries: Amazon's two guides contradict each other.** The 2021 UK guide describes "Cross-country Copy-as-draft"; the 2023 US guide says "It is not currently possible to copy content from one country to another." [official] (both guides) The current help pages settle it (gap fill): "Note: It is not currently possible to copy content from one country to another. You will have to recreate the A+ page from the designated account." [official] (Create A+ content, G202134820 v17; Edit A+ content, G7DAP8D85ATMUD7Q v1; both read 2026-10-14) The 2026 Quickguide says only that you can "duplicate existing content to use it as a template … or to translate the content into other languages for other Amazon marketplaces where you sell". The 2023 US guide also says "Amazon may create language variations automatically for designated products."

### The SP-API A+ Content Management API (exists; what it can do)

- **Version, scope and sandbox.** "2020-11-01 … Legacy versions: None … Availability: Sellers and Vendors … Sandbox: Static". Each of its document operations needs at least one of the roles Brand Analytics or Product Listing. [official] (A+ Content API)
- **Operations** (model paths, `/aplus/2020-11-01/…`):
  - `searchContentDocuments` (metadata only);
  - `getContentDocument` (`includedDataSet=CONTENTS,METADATA`, comma-separated: "only the first is kept" if repeated);
  - `createContentDocument`;
  - `updateContentDocument`;
  - `listContentDocumentAsinRelations`;
  - `postContentDocumentAsinRelations`;
  - `validateContentDocumentAsinRelations` (`POST /contentAsinValidations`, `asinSet` as a comma-separated query parameter);
  - `searchContentPublishRecords` (`asin` required);
  - `postContentDocumentApprovalSubmission`;
  - `postContentDocumentSuspendSubmission` ("This neither deletes the content document nor the ASIN relations");
  - `createMedia`, `getMedia`, `updateMedia`.

  [official] (model; API reference index https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/llms.txt; Troubleshooting Guide)
- **The workflow Amazon prescribes:**
  1. Read what is published (`searchContentPublishRecords`).
  2. Read all documents (`searchContentDocuments`).
  3. Build the document.
  4. Get an upload destination from the Uploads API (`createUploadDestinationForResource`) and POST the image to the pre-signed URL.
  5. Validate (`validateContentDocumentAsinRelations`) "Repeat until you receive no errors".
  6. Create.
  7. Post the ASINs.
  8. Submit.
  9. Poll status.

  [official] (Create, edit, and publish A+ content)
- **The image upload leaves the SP-API host.** It goes to a pre-signed S3 URL (the example is `https://aplus-media.s3.amazonaws.com/`). [official] (Create, edit, and publish A+ content)
- **Media statuses.** The A+ media library has `PENDING_PROCESSING`, `PENDING_REVIEW`, `UNABLE_TO_PROCESS`, `NOT_APPROVED`, `AVAILABLE`, `PENDING_AMAZON` and `PENDING_SELLING_PARTNER`. "The `mediaUrl` value is non-permanent." [official] (Create, Edit, and Retrieve A+ Media Assets, https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/create-edit-retrieve-aplus-media, read 2026-10-14)
- **Rate limits: Amazon's pages disagree.** The rate-limits page prints per-operation rates from 5 to 20 per second (for example 20 for `getContentDocument`, 5 for `postContentDocumentSuspendSubmission`) and "Per-developer aggregate: 30". The model and the reference pages print rate 10, burst 10 for every operation. [official] (A+ Content API Rate Limits, https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/aplus-api-rate-limits, page updatedAt 2026-09-09, re-read 2026-10-14; aplusContent_2020-11-01.json, re-read 2026-10-14; reference pages, read 2026-10-14) The seat does not choose between them. The model declares the `x-amzn-RateLimit-Limit` response header on its operations, so the binding rate is read at run time from that header; the figures belong to door.md, and no skill line quotes one.
- **A+ is absent from the other APIs.** No other model read carries A+. The Catalog Items 2022-04-01 and Listings Items 2021-08-01 models contain no "aplus" or "A+" string, and the Data Kiosk seller analytics schema has no A+ field (string search of each model, 2026-10-14). [official, by absence]

### Search, discoverability, AI shopping

- **Amazon has never said in a current page read that A+ text is indexed by Amazon search. It has said things close to it.**
  - The 2021 UK guide: "FREE FORM TEXT = IMPROVES SEO. Text and Image Keywords are indexed by Search Engines." The sentence does not say which search engines.
  - The 2023 US guide, under "Improve Discoverability": "Use text fields across modules, limit the amount of text embedded in images, and use descriptive Image Keywords (alt-text) for each of your uploaded images."
  - The News_Amazon thread "Improve your listings with A+ Content best practices" (dated only "a year ago") repeats the "Improve discoverability" advice.

  [official] (Complete Guide UK; Complete Guide US; Seller Forums https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/969ad286-38ec-4c44-8851-14dcdf7c99c2, read 2026-10-14) None of these says "Amazon search ranks on A+ text". The lead "Amazon says not indexed?" is **not confirmed**: the seat found no Amazon page that says either way about Amazon's own search.
  - **Gap fill: three current Seller Central lines come closer.**
    - On alt text: "Alt-text does not show on the product detail page, but is used by visually impaired customers and screen reader apps, and helps your products in search results." [official] (Create A+ content, G202134820 v17, read 2026-10-14) The sentence does not say which search results, nor whether A+ body text is treated the same way.

#### Category claims and takedowns

- **The seller A+ guideline page (GGW8U76SSNTRTBX7) adds to the first pass's list:**
  - "Do not mention awards received more than 2 years ago."
  - Certification claims "(for example, recommended by, certified, tested, approved, proven, validated) and awards must be substantiated by a note in the text with certifying or awarding body, study, publication or other evidence, and year."
  - "Environmental claims with words like "eco-friendly," "biodegradable," and "compostable," including their symbols, are not allowed either in text or in images." "Recyclable" or "ecological" need a substantiating note.
  - "Remove any claims to cure, mitigate, treat, or prevent diseases in humans or animals or both regarding drugs, cosmetics, medical devices, beverages, food, and health products (including natural health products, vitamins, and supplements)."
  - "A maximum of four quotes or endorsements is allowed", only from "well-known publications or public figures"; no quotes from "individuals, customers, or other private figures".
  - "Comparison charts may only compare against other products owned by the same brand. Comparisons made against categories, like LED bulbs vs halogen ones, are acceptable and not considered competitive call-outs."
  - Competitors may not be referenced "regardless of how competitors are mentioned (for example, specific names, described as "other(s)", or otherwise)".
  - No "buy now", "add to cart"; no seller or distributor references ("product only sold by authorized resellers"); no off-Amazon return or refund policies; images ".jpg, .bmp, and .png in the RGB colorspace", "under 2 MB", animated images "prohibited"; "Images and text must be unique to A+. ## Published research

- **Reddy, Màrquez, Valero, Rao, Zaragoza, Bandyopadhyay, Biswas, Xing, Subbian, "Shopping Queries Dataset: A Large-Scale ESCI Benchmark for Improving Product Search", arXiv preprint arXiv:2206.06588, 2022 (the dataset was used in the KDD Cup 2022 challenge; the arXiv page lists no publication venue).** [research] The finding, narrowly: Amazon's public search-relevance benchmark (~130k queries, 2.6M judged query-product pairs) represents a product by `product_title`, `product_description`, `product_bullet_point`, `product_brand` and `product_color` (repository https://github.com/amazon-science/esci-data, column list). It has no A+ field. **Transfer:** weak. - **Gap fill: Zhang, Lee, Singh, Srinivasan, "What Makes a Good Image? Airbnb Demand Analytics Leveraging Interpretable Image Features", *Management Science*, 2022 (DOI 10.1287/mnsc.2021.4175).** [research] The abstract was read through the Crossref record (api.crossref.org/works/10.1287/mnsc.2021.4175, read 2026-10-14). It is an observational study of gallery images on a peer-to-peer marketplace, not of below-the-fold brand modules; it supports only the general idea that listing images relate to sales.

## Stable enough for a skill

1. A+ is a conversion-stage element: it renders on the detail page (Product Description / From the Manufacturer for A+; From the Brand for Brand Story), not in search results. (A+ Premium Modules; A+ Brand Story Modules)
2. A+ is not a Listings Items attribute. It is read and written through the A+ Content Management API (`/aplus/2020-11-01/…`), and its images go through the Uploads API to a pre-signed URL. (A+ Content API; Create, edit, and publish A+ content)
3. A+ requires a Professional account and a Brand Registry brand role (or generic products), and can be applied only to brand-owned ASINs; Amazon enforces this at create/submit, not at validation. (A+ Content | Sell on Amazon; A+ Content Troubleshooting Guide)
4. A document is all Standard, all Premium or all Brand Story; the three never mix. (A+ Premium Modules; A+ Brand Story Modules; Troubleshooting Guide)
5. A 200 from validation or from posting ASINs is not a pass; the `errors`, `warnings` and ASIN badges are. (Create, edit, and publish A+ content; Troubleshooting Guide)
6. Status runs DRAFT → SUBMITTED → APPROVED or REJECTED. Updating or re-posting ASINs on an approved document returns it to DRAFT while the approved revision stays live. (model; Troubleshooting Guide)
7. Only one product-description A+ document and one Brand Story can be live per ASIN. Approving another silently replaces the live one, and posting an ASIN set replaces the whole set. (Quickguide; Manage A+ content; Troubleshooting Guide)
8. Approval is not publication: moderation can take days, and publication can lag approval. The current figures are in Amazon's guide and may change. (Complete Guide 2023 US; Troubleshooting Guide)
9. A live A+ document hides the plain-text description on the detail page. (Complete Guide UK 2021; A+ Content help page)
10. A+ shows only in the language it was written for. (Complete Guide 2023 US)
11. Amazon's content rules for A+ forbid pricing, promotions, shipping details, links, contact details, guarantee/warranty text, unsubstantiated superlatives and any reference to competitors. Comparison modules compare the brand's own ASINs. (How to create A+ Content…; A+ Premium Modules; A+ Content Guidelines, KDP)
12. Amazon's A+ sales-lift claim is its own observational marketing figure (footnoted "Amazon internal data", or a comparison of products with and without A+ Content). The figure has changed from one Amazon document to the next, so it is quoted only as printed on the page read at the time, with its footnote. It is not an expected effect for a given ASIN. (A+ Content | Sell on Amazon; Quickguide; Complete Guides UK 2021 and US 2023)
13. Manage Your Experiments can A/B test A+ and Brand Story once one is published. Its auto-publish option covers title, image and bullet-point experiments only, so an A+ winner goes live only when the seller publishes it in A+ Content Manager. (Test listing content with Manage Your Experiments; Create a listing experiment; Experiment results) *Corrected in the gap fill; the first pass said the winner is auto-published by default.*
14. Amazon does not state that A+ body text is indexed by Amazon search, and does not name A+ among Alexa for Shopping's (formerly Rufus) sources. It does say that A+ image alt text "helps your products in search results", and that Alexa for Shopping answers product questions from "the product's detail page". (Complete Guide UK/US; Amazon announces Rufus…; The technology behind… Rufus; Create A+ content; Alexa for shopping)
15. Premium A+ eligibility is set by Amazon and re-evaluated by Amazon on a schedule; the current criteria and the fee status are stated on the Seller Central "A+ Content" help page and shown to the seller as a banner in A+ Content Manager. (A+ Content help page)
16. A+ contributed by an Amazon retail vendor (From the manufacturer) takes precedence over seller A+ (Product description), and a seller cannot add or edit A+ on such an ASIN. (A+ Content help page; A+ content FAQ & troubleshooting)
17. Amazon offers no A+-specific sales or traffic metric in any SP-API model or Data Kiosk dataset read. The quality score lives in A+ Content Manager's "Content quality analysis" tab, and the only causal measure is a Manage Your Experiments result, whose conversion rate counts visitors who saw the A+ content. (A+ Content help page; A+ content FAQ & troubleshooting; Experiment results; aplusContent_2020-11-01.json; the Data Kiosk schemas)
18. Data Kiosk `conversionRatePct` counts only search-originated clicks and purchases; unit session percentage (units ordered over all sessions) is the detail-page conversion figure. (analytics_sellerAnalytics_2025_03_31.graphql; analytics_salesAndTraffic_2024_04_24.graphql)
19. Amazon may display A+ collapsed by default and links expanded display to its quality assessment. (A+ content FAQ & troubleshooting)
20. Amazon may create A+ language variations, and translate and publish A+ into other countries, without the seller acting. Content from an automated process carries the `GENERATED` badge until a user edits it. (Create A+ content; aplusContent_2020-11-01.json)
21. Claims rules for the product's category apply to A+, and an A+ violation can lead to removal of the ASIN, not only rejection of the content. (A+ content guidelines; Misleading and prohibited claims)
22. A+ text, image keywords and text inside A+ images are checked against a prohibited-keyword list, and a hit blocks submission; the text is also subject to manual review. (A+ content FAQ & troubleshooting)
23. Seller A+ cannot be copied from one country to another; it is recreated in the other account. (Create A+ content; Edit A+ content)

## What cx must read at run time

- **Which A+ is live on an ASIN:** `searchContentPublishRecords` (`asin`, `marketplaceId`). The result gives `contentReferenceKey`, `contentType` (family) and `locale`. Then `getContentDocument` with `includedDataSet=CONTENTS,METADATA` gives the modules, `status`, `badgeSet` (tier: `PREMIUM`/`STANDARD`) and `updateTime`. The publish record carries no publish timestamp (model `PublishRecord`). Paths and the call shape are door.md's.
- **All of the seller's A+ documents:** `searchContentDocuments` (metadata only).
- **Which ASINs a document covers, and which are unusable:** `listContentDocumentAsinRelations` with `includedDataSet=METADATA`, reading the badges `CONTENT_PUBLISHED`, `CONTENT_NOT_PUBLISHED`, `BRAND_NOT_ELIGIBLE` and `CATALOG_NOT_FOUND`, plus `warnings`.
- **The limits a draft must meet:** not stated in the skill. cx runs `validateContentDocumentAsinRelations` and reads `errors`/`warnings`. That is the A+ counterpart of Listings `VALIDATION_PREVIEW`, and it persists nothing (Troubleshooting Guide: "You can run the dry run… before persisting a document").
- **After a submission:** `getContentDocument` `status`, no more than once an hour per document.
- **Competitor A+ and own A+ as shoppers see it:** Keepa product `aPlus` (requested with the `aplus` parameter; `cx raw keepa` `/product` lists `aplus` among its parameters, per the Keepa skill's raw-routes.md). Keepa's backend notes: "To request live data for this field, the `offers` parameter must also be included. Returns `null` if unavailable." (keepacom/api_backend `Product.java`, read 2026-10-14) No A+ history field exists in that struct. This limitation may change in the future without prior notice." (Keepa Product Object, https://keepa.com/api-docs/product-object.html). The seat does not choose; no skill line states either number, and cx treats a Keepa description as possibly cut (description.md's open question 7 holds the comparison that settles it).
- **The effect of an A+ change:** the conversion figures of the targeted child ASIN(s), on all detail-page sessions (gap fill, corrected):
  - `unitSessionPercentage`, `sessions` and units ordered in `GET_SALES_AND_TRAFFIC_REPORT` (by ASIN across a range);
  - or the Data Kiosk Sales and Traffic dataset (`analytics_salesAndTraffic_2024_04_24`, `salesAndTrafficByAsin`, `ByAsinTraffic.unitSessionPercentage`, defined as "unitsOrdered by sessions");
  - or `unitsOrdered` over `sessions` from Data Kiosk `analytics_sellerAnalytics_2025_03_31`, with the browser/mobile-app split (`browserSessions`, `mobileAppSessions`) when the device mix moved.
  - **Not** Data Kiosk `conversionRatePct` (nor its deprecated twin `conversationRatePct`): the schema defines it as "the percentage of purchases to clicks for ASINs originated from the search results page", so it leaves out every non-search session A+ also acts on. The first pass offered it as an alternative; the gap fill withdraws that. (Data Kiosk fields carry `@resultRetention(duration: "P30D")`, so a result document is kept 30 days.)
  - For a Brand Story or comparison-chart change, the same figures for the brand's other ASINs (Data Kiosk seller analytics groups by `brand` and `parentAsin`), since the cross-sell lands there.

  Report names, options and waits are door.md's. No A+-specific metric exists in any SP-API model read.

## Positions

1. **P-a-plus-content-1.** A+ (and Brand Story) is a conversion-stage element, so a review looks at A+ only after it has located the drop in conversion. It never offers A+ as the answer to an exposure or click-through drop. (A+ Premium Modules; A+ Brand Story Modules; A+ Content quick guide)
2. **P-a-plus-content-2.** cx reads and writes A+ only through the A+ Content Management API (`/aplus/2020-11-01/…`), never through a Listings Items patch. (A+ Content API; Create, edit, and publish A+ content; aplusContent_2020-11-01.json)
3. **P-a-plus-content-3.** Before proposing A+ work, cx establishes eligibility (Brand Registry role, brand-owned ASIN, no retail contribution) and treats a 403 at submit as an eligibility answer, not a content fault. (A+ Content | Sell on Amazon; A+ Content Troubleshooting Guide)
4. **P-a-plus-content-4.** cx reads what is live with `searchContentPublishRecords` and then `getContentDocument`, and records the live `contentReferenceKey` as the "before" of any A+ change. (Create, edit, and publish A+ content; Troubleshooting Guide)
5. **P-a-plus-content-5.** cx judges a draft by the `errors` and `warnings` of `validateContentDocumentAsinRelations` and by the ASIN badges, never by an HTTP 200. (Create, edit, and publish A+ content; Troubleshooting Guide)
6. **P-a-plus-content-6.** The change log records an A+ change with these fields:
   - the content reference key;
   - the full ASIN set posted;
   - the submission time;
   - each observed status with its time;
   - the time cx first saw it published.

   The observation window opens at publication, not at submission. (Create, edit, and publish A+ content; Complete Guide 2023 US; Troubleshooting Guide)
7. **P-a-plus-content-7.** cx warns the seller in four situations:
   - submitting a new document for an ASIN silently replaces the live one;
   - posting an ASIN set replaces the whole set;
   - editing an approved document sends it back to review;
   - the old version stays live until the new one is approved.

   (Manage A+ content; Troubleshooting Guide; A+ Content quick guide)
8. **P-a-plus-content-8.** When an ASIN has live A+, cx does not expect an edit to the plain-text `product_description` to change what shoppers see. (Complete Guide UK 2021)
9. **P-a-plus-content-9.** cx never proposes A+ copy that does any of the following:
   - states price, promotion, shipping or warranty;
   - names or compares a competitor;
   - quotes reviews;
   - uses unsubstantiated superlatives;
   - carries links or contact details.

   Comparison modules compare only the brand's own ASINs. (How to create A+ Content: Design tips…; A+ Premium Modules; A+ Content Guidelines, KDP)
10. **P-a-plus-content-10.** cx quotes Amazon's A+ sales-lift claim only as printed on the Amazon page it reads at the time, with its footnote, as Amazon's observational marketing figure, never as the expected effect of a seller's change. (A+ Content | Sell on Amazon; A+ Content quick guide) *Reconciled 2026-10-14: the figure is no longer written into the position, since Amazon has changed it across documents.*
11. **P-a-plus-content-11.** cx does not claim that A+ body text helps Amazon search ranking or that Alexa for Shopping reads A+, since Amazon states neither. It may say that Amazon describes A+ image alt text as helping "your products in search results", quoted as Amazon's own words. (Complete Guide UK/US; Amazon announces Rufus…; The technology behind… Rufus; Create A+ content; Alexa for shopping) *Amended in the gap fill.*
12. **P-a-plus-content-12.** cx judges an A+ change on the targeted ASINs' unit session percentage over all sessions (Sales and Traffic report or the Data Kiosk Sales and Traffic dataset), together with orders, using the same source in baseline and window. It never uses Data Kiosk `conversionRatePct`, which counts only search-originated clicks and so cannot isolate a detail-page module. (sellerSalesAndTrafficReport.json; analytics_salesAndTraffic_2024_04_24.graphql; analytics_sellerAnalytics_2025_03_31.graphql; aplusContent_2020-11-01.json) *Corrected in the gap fill; the first pass allowed `conversionRatePct` as an alternative.*
13. **P-a-plus-content-13.** cx treats a running Manage Your Experiments test on the ASIN as a confounder, because it splits what shoppers see. When a test is running, cx gives no verdict on another change to the same ASIN in that window. When an A+ test ends, cx treats the seller's publication of the winner as a new change, since A+ winners are not auto-published. (Test listing content with Manage Your Experiments; Create a listing experiment; Experiment results) *Corrected in the gap fill.*
14. **P-a-plus-content-14.** cx reads competitors' A+ through Keepa's `aPlus` field and treats it as a current snapshot with no history. (keepacom/api_backend Product.java; Keepa skill raw-routes.md)
15. **P-a-plus-content-15.** Before proposing Premium A+ modules (video, hotspots, Premium comparison, Q&A), cx checks Premium access: the A+ Content Manager banner, as reported by the seller, or the refusal on submission. ### The seat's own (not for a skill)

- S-1. When A+ is in moderation, the "change" is not yet on the page. An effect-review that dates the change at submission will read up to a week of unchanged content as "no effect". Inferred from Amazon's stated review and publish lags.
- S-2. One A+ document often spans a whole variation family or a product line. A single A+ change therefore touches several child ASINs at once, and the effect-review's "one change at a time" may need the ASIN set as its unit. Inferred, not sourced.
- S-3. The day-2 zero-playbook run is evidence that, without a method, cx conflates A+ with Listings attributes. The listing-review skill should name the A+ door explicitly. Internal run report; no Amazon source.
- S-4. Amazon's own lift figures are likely biased upward as a causal estimate. Brands that invest in A+ probably differ from those that do not, and the footnote's "vs no A+ Content" comparison does not control for that. This is the seat's reading; no source states it.
- S-5. The comparison modules require an ASIN per column, and Amazon's Quickguide calls Basic's comparison charts "shoppable product-comparison charts" and Premium "shoppable content". They may move some sessions to sibling ASINs, which would look like a conversion drop on the targeted ASIN while brand-level sales rise. The effect is unverified.
- S-6. The day-2 zero-playbook proposal included an A+ comparison table framed against a rival class of product to escape a price comparison with a competitor. Amazon's blog says "Never reference or compare products with competitors", and the Premium comparison modules are documented for "products in your catalog". A method that does not carry the content rules would propose content likely to be rejected. Internal run report; the rule itself is sourced above.
- S-7 (gap fill). The A+ quality score in "Content quality analysis" includes "conversion effectiveness". A seller who edits A+ to raise that score may also change whether Amazon shows the content expanded. The score and the display state could then move conversion together, and an effect-review could not separate them. Inferred from the FAQ's wording; not tested.
- S-8 (gap fill). Amazon's "3.6X more likely to convert" for video viewers compares shoppers who chose to watch with shoppers who did not. Like the 8%/20% lift, it is not an expected effect of adding a video. The seat's reading; Amazon gives no method.
- S-9 (gap fill). Measuring a Brand Story at the brand level (summing the brand's ASINs) will pick up every other change across the brand in the same window. The one-change-at-a-time rule may be unattainable for Brand Story unless the seller freezes other brand-wide changes. Inference.
