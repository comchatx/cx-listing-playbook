# Playbook defaults: what the company's own rulings leave to the playbook

Read 2026-11-04 for brief 2026-11-03-54. Tags as in `research/README.md`. This file holds no Amazon rule of its own. It records three lines that ComChatX's charter once held as rules. On 2026-08-27 Jack moved them out of the charter and into the playbook as defaults the model may set aside. Where a line rests on Amazon's words, the Amazon item is named and the file that holds it.

## What this covers

- How a series is shown after a chart.
- What A+ is for, and what Amazon has said of its text in search, as of the date read.
- Which language a listing's text is written in.

## The ruling

The CEO office's worksheet `2026-08-27-ai-constraint-recut-worksheet.md`, section 二, lists five charter lines to recut, R1 to R5, with Jack's note under the table: 「以上全部都能删」 (all of the above may be deleted). Three of them belong to this playbook:
- **R2**, the charter's "after a chart, a key-timepoint table (≤ 6 rows) + insight". The worksheet's reading: how such an analysis is shown is the playbook's to say, and the model picks the form for the case.
- **R3**, "A+ serves conversion, never SEO". The worksheet's reading: Amazon's indexing of A+ text is a platform fact that changes. The playbook keeps it as knowledge, with its source and date, not as a ban.
- **R4**, "target-market language". The worksheet's reading: a playbook default that the user's own words override, for example a seller who wants both languages.

The worksheet lives in the office's tree, outside this repository.

## Positions

- **P-playbook-defaults-1.** After a chart, a table of its key time points (at most six rows) and one insight is this playbook's default way to show a series. It is a default, not a format rule, and the model may choose another form that suits the question. Sources: worksheet 2026-08-27 §二 R2, with Jack's note.
- **P-playbook-defaults-2.** A listing's text is written by default in each store's own language. The seller's own word overrides that default, for example a seller who asks for two languages. Sources: worksheet 2026-08-27 §二 R4, with Jack's note.
- **P-playbook-defaults-3.** That A+ serves conversion is playbook knowledge, not a ban on what A+ text may say. Whether Amazon search reads A+ text is a dated platform fact, as funnel-and-search.md holds it. Sources: worksheet 2026-08-27 §二 R3, with Jack's note; P-a-plus-content-1; P-funnel-and-search-30.
